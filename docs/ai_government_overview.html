⚔️ How is it used compared to usual AI tools?

Most people use LLMs for chatbots or free-wheeling autonomous agents. EcoSim turns the paradigm upside down.

Typical AI Agent
🤖 "The Direct Tool Caller"

Common in chat assistants, code agents, and open-ended bot frameworks.

🛑 Freezes the World (Synchronous)
The entire game loop stops and waits 3 seconds while OpenAI or Anthropic streams tokens. The simulation stutters to 0 FPS.
👁️ God-Mode Vision
Fed 100% accurate, instant database values. Real governments never have instant perfect data!
💥 Direct Execution Authority
The LLM executes SQL queries or functions directly. If it hallucinates tax = 9999%, the country implodes.
EcoSim Architecture
🛡️ "Constrained Feedback Controller"

Treats the LLM as an un-trusted policy advisor wrapped inside mathematical guardrails.

⚡ Decoupled Clocks (Asynchronous)
Takes a frozen snapshot at Tick 26. Sim continues stepping at 50ms. AI delivers advice at Tick 29. Zero lag or freezing.
🌫️ Realistic Fog of War
GDP is lagged by 3 ticks. Unemployment is lagged by 2 ticks with 5% survey noise. Gini index reports drop out randomly.
🧱 The Deterministic Firewall
AI only returns JSON proposals. Code clamps tax changes to ±5%, limits moves to 2 per cycle, and enforces cash reserve floors.

The Self-Driving Analogy for CS Students:

The LLM is like a passenger suggesting navigation routes: "Turn left here to avoid traffic!". The deterministic Python code is the vehicle's Anti-Lock Braking System and Steering Lockout. If the passenger yells "Turn left into that concrete barrier at 90 MPH!", the code simply says "Command rejected: physical boundary exceeded." The car stays safe on the road.

🔬 How it is implemented in apply_info_constraints_node:

Indicators are impaired according to INDICATOR_CONSTRAINTS. A deterministic pseudo-random number generator (seeded by seed + tick + indicator) injects Gaussian survey noise (e.g. ±8% wage variation) and lag (GDP reads from 3 ticks ago). If a survey sample drops below the coverage threshold, the indicator is passed as "status": "unavailable", forcing the LLM to govern under uncertainty.

⏱️ Why Two Clocks? (The Non-Blocking Secret)

How EcoSim reconciles a 50ms simulation clock with a 2,000ms LLM reasoning clock.

Fast Clock: Simulation Engine (Ticks 26 → 27 → 28 → 29) Speed: ~50ms per tick
Tick 26
Snapshot taken
Tick 27
Households consume
Tick 28
Firms pay wages
Tick 29
Advice arrives!
Asyncio Background Task (Deep-Copied Economy)
Slow Clock: AI Decision Cycle (~1,500ms total elapsed) Provider API Call
1. Prompt Assembled 2. Network Inference (Groq/Ollama) 3. Firewall Vetting
Observed: Tick 26 Applied: Tick 29
Why this matters: If inference takes 4 seconds because of network congestion, the economy doesn't freeze. The simulation keeps running happily. When the AI finishes, the engine checks if the advice is still legal and applies it at the nearest tick boundary.

🎛️ The 17 Macroeconomic Levers

The exact policy controls the AI central government can adjust.

Taxes

wage_tax_rate

0.0 – 0.50

Taxes household wages directly on payday.

▲ Increases government cash
▼ Reduces consumer retail spending
Rule: Max change ±0.05 per cycle
Taxes

profit_tax_rate

0.0 – 0.50

Taxes business net profits at the end of each cycle.

▲ High revenue without hitting workers
▼ Firms have less cash buffer for hiring
Rule: Max change ±0.05 per cycle
Taxes

investment_tax_rate

0.0 – 0.30

Taxes firm capital expenditures & R&D budgets.

▲ Cools speculative asset bubbles
▼ Slows long-term product quality growth
Rule: Capped strictly at 30%
Welfare

benefit_level

low→crisis

Unemployment stimulus transfers sent to jobless citizens.

▲ Prevents starvation & sustains food sales
▼ High fiscal drain; raises reservation wages
Rule: 1-step move only (e.g. low → neutral)
Welfare

minimum_wage_policy

low|neutral|high

Legal hourly floor businesses must pay staff.

▲ Boosts minimum standard of living
▼ Fragile firms halt recruitment or lay off
Rule: 1-step movement per cycle
Mega Projects

public_works

off | on

Direct state emergency hiring for unemployed citizens.

▲ Slashes massive unemployment fast
▼ Extremely expensive startup capital
Rule: Requires unemp ≥ 25% + reserve funds
