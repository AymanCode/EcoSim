**Status: proposal round and coordinator review complete; implementation not started.** Six independent Sol/high writers produced 17 proposal IDs. An additional Sol/high reviewer checked representative primary evidence for all six roles. The coordinator traced consequential claims and shared interfaces against the working tree recorded in [ROUND_MANIFEST.json](ROUND_MANIFEST.json). No simulation, experiment or benchmark was run. Source tracing establishes what inspected paths do; it does not establish their aggregate economic effects.

Read the [short index](README.md) first. The six original proposals are preserved as submitted, including claims qualified below. This review supplies corrections and integration conditions; an implementation brief must incorporate them rather than copy an original proposal alone. Existing agent rules remain v1.1. A proposal requesting a shared-rule change does not itself change those rules.

## Review standard

A realistic abstraction preserves the important decision, constraint, timing and feedback for the policy question. It names observations that could support or contradict it and explains why omitted detail is acceptable. ODD recommends documenting purpose, evaluation patterns, state, scheduling and model rationale; these principles guide this review without implying formal ODD compliance. [Grimm et al., 2020](https://www.jasss.org/23/2/7.html).

Mechanism plausibility, implementation correctness, parameter evidence and suitability for the intended use are separate questions. TRACE distinguishes development and evaluation stages; it is methodological guidance originally developed for ecological models, not evidence for a particular economic response. [Grimm et al., 2014](https://faculty.sites.iastate.edu/tesfatsi/archive/tesfatsi/TRACE.ModFramework.GrimmEtAl2014.pdf).

The coordinator assessed each document before cross-role integration. **Revise** means a useful candidate needs the listed specification correction; **needs evidence/choice** means a behavioral or institutional assumption remains open; **defer** means an earlier interface is required. These are design dispositions, not code approval. No effect size, universal behavioral coefficient, real-world forecast or runtime compliance is validated by this round.

## Source corrections and qualifications

**IR01 — A sampled trait is not necessarily active.** FIRM's baseline says its sampled R&D traits all have live readers. The `rd_spending_rate` personality field is assigned, but actual [R&D spending](../../../backend/agents.py#L5644) uses configured base/scaling/cap rates and realized margin. The claimed trait-to-spending connection is absent in that path. Preserve existing live heterogeneity; assigning another field does not make behavior unique. This does not invalidate demand-gated investment.

**IR02 — Keep return and liquidity tests distinct.** FIRM-P01 compares contribution with capital cost “and scheduled debt service.” Specify incremental operating costs and capital opportunity cost in the return test; handle principal repayment and existing commitments in a separate cash-availability test. Principal is a balance-sheet repayment, not an additional production expense. Otherwise the same financing cost can suppress investment twice. Replacing the existing decision rule is a modeling choice, not an accounting identity alone.

**IR03 — A self-financed firm can still react to rates.** BANK-P03's example says a firm with abundant self-finance is unaffected. [The current investment hurdle](../../../backend/agents.py#L4452) reads the bank base rate before choosing cash versus loan finance. Such a firm has no new-loan payment exposure, but its investment decision can change through this opportunity-cost proxy. Do not use debt-free firms as an automatically untreated control group.

**IR04 — Separate capital routes, including a reset hazard.** [Early long-term expansion](../../../backend/economy.py#L1615) occurs before [ordinary capital planning](../../../backend/economy.py#L1673). The early route immediately adds service capacity or housing units and [adds a capital-recycling counter](../../../backend/economy.py#L5109); the later planner [resets that counter to zero](../../../backend/agents.py#L4433). The inspected sequence can erase the early route's intended recycled payment before [phase-8.5 distribution](../../../backend/economy.py#L5371). This is a concrete source-order finding, not an executed balance reconciliation. Specify that payment once before changing capacity timing. FIRM-P01 alone would not govern all expansions; HOUSE-P03 must cover self-funding, early ordinary bank loans and separate housing mortgages. New public projects must not reuse this route without resolving its counter and counterparty semantics.

**IR05 — Housing has two asking-price writes and a duplicated mortgage input.** [Rental clearing](../../../backend/economy.py#L5983) caps a posted-price increase, then [phase 9](../../../backend/economy.py#L1949) applies an earlier price plan. Treat the cap as effective only after reconciling those writers. [Mortgage origination](../../../backend/economy.py#L5257) mirrors PMT into `bank_loan_payment_per_tick`; [housing pricing](../../../backend/agents.py#L4568) also sums active mortgage PMTs. That can count the same mortgage twice in its cost signal, although it does not prove duplicate cash collection. Audit contract identity before subtracting any amount. `monthly_rent` is paid per weekly tick; HOUSING's reference to a “phase-8 household batch path” should read phase 10, where household outcomes settle.

**IR06 — Expected income and deposit balances are not guaranteed payment.** [Household planning](../../../backend/economy.py#L736) precedes phase-2a new consumption credit, rent, care, registered debt collection and phase-10 wages. HH-P02 correctly excludes later credit unless a coordinated replan is selected. Goods are allocated and firm receipts credited before the household purchase debit; changing a budget alone cannot reconcile a later affordability adjustment with seller revenue and inventory. Define one reservation/settlement contract across these owners, including payment priority. The bank must return actual withdrawals; households cannot independently reserve the same shared reserves.

**IR07 — Fiscal earmarks are not automatically creditor liabilities.** GOV-P01 describes project funds as encumbered treasury cash, but also calls an allocation a “liability/encumbrance.” An internal earmark restricts usable cash; record a payable only when a specified counterparty has a claim. Do not create a second asset or debt by earmarking. Define eligible receipts separately from closing cash so already paid transfers/subsidies are not deducted twice. The illustrative “extra 100” is explanatory arithmetic, not an available online counterfactual revenue signal.

**IR08 — Holding a fiscal rule fixed does not hold spending fixed.** GOV-P01's reserve fallback can still change cash-capped subsidies and automatic surplus-to-pool spending. It supports a tax comparison under that stated rule, not an isolated private disposable-income experiment. CARE-P02, HOUSE-P02 relief and GOV-P02 cannot each earmark the same treasury balance. Compare one financing closure at a time and report both tax incidence and induced public outlays.

**IR09 — A clinic room is a proposed resource.** GOV-P02's room example introduces a facility constraint absent from [current visit capacity](../../../backend/economy.py#L6219), which primarily sums worker skills and has a nominal fallback. Paying for rooms does not create doctors. CARE-P03's qualification correction is separable from adding buildings, training seats or procurement. For any delivered asset, specify its supplier, consumed capacity and whether it adds a new bottleneck.

**IR10 — Freeze need only in the isolated care test.** CARE-P01's test that a charge change “never” changes latent illness counts applies to a same-state, single-decision check. Across a run, changed treatment changes health and therefore later episode probabilities. Pair initial state and exogenous random inputs, but allow that endogenous feedback. Keep denial, deferral, unmet clinical need and physical waiting distinct. The cited Oregon labor result does not establish a general health-to-output benefit.

**IR11 — Delays and grace periods need failure rules.** HOUSE-P02 must distinguish current rent from old arrears and specify payment allocation; no write-off creates cash. HOUSE-P03 needs an unfinished-project rule at provider exit, including lender claims and prepaid construction proxies. GOV-P02 needs corresponding cancellation/refund rules. Do not activate a new claim while FIRM-P02's exit path still ignores it.

**IR12 — Complexity and provenance claims need qualification.** O(H + L) debt aggregation and O(F + L) exit resolution require indexed/pass-based views; existing repeated per-borrower loan searches do not establish those costs. No benchmark was run. HEALTHCARE's inline `backend/economy.py` SHA is truncated; the full authoritative hash is in the manifest. That metadata typo does not identify a different source version. Proposal hashes preserve originals; this review records corrections rather than silently rewriting them.

## Recommended next design sequence

1. **Concrete source and accounting specifications:** IR04's capital-payment reset; IR05's rent-price and mortgage-cost ownership; qualified-care capacity; BANK-P01 schedule/funder conventions; FIRM-P02 funding/exit claims. These are bounded source problems, although contract changes still need an explicit convention and later targeted tests. Preserve tax progression; bracket redesign is not the priority.
2. **One shared resource and fiscal contract:** finish HH-P02, BANK-P02 deposit access and GOV-P01's cash/earmark rule together. Define paid versus due versus expected amounts and phase ownership. This unlocks coherent propagation without a new actor or expensive search network.
3. **Two focused policy-design packages:** for tax, combine expected take-home planning with declared revenue use and demand-aware investment; for healthcare, combine a specified payer contract with charge-sensitive requests and qualified capacity. Specify and eventually test components separately before combined arms. Different financing rules are different interventions.
4. **Institutional extensions after the above:** net job-gain decisions/private wage rules, lease renewal and arrears, delayed housing and selected public projects. Keep endogenous medical training and broad monetary/regime modeling separate until their omitted resource and balance-sheet rules are chosen.

Capitalism/socialism/communism should later be represented through explicit ownership, price-setting, investment, allocation, labor and financing rules. This round does not supply those regime specifications. Inflation comparisons still need a defined price index and an implemented monetary or supply/demand mechanism; a stored target cannot substitute for either. No mechanism should be rewarded for producing a preferred GDP, inequality or ideological result.

## Evidence audit and completion boundary

A separate Sol/high reviewer checked representative original studies or author/institution pages for each role. Household liquidity evidence does not validate bank-withdrawal restrictions; firm expectation evidence does not identify a weekly investment coefficient; stimulus receipt evidence does not identify an announcement effect. Banking sources support explicitly distinguishing the prefunded model from modern deposit creation. Healthcare evidence separates use/financial protection from labor outcomes. These qualifications are consistent with the original proposals' stated limits.

The housing qualification is particularly consequential: the [San Francisco study](https://web.stanford.edu/~diamondr/DMQOld.pdf) includes conversion/redevelopment and eviction-regulation channels missing from a simple renewal cap. Preserve its finding as context, not a predicted supply decline in EcoSim. [Census construction-duration tables](https://www.census.gov/construction/c30/length.html) support distinguishing start and completion, not a selected synthetic delay. This was a representative check, not replication or verification of every citation; NBER access restrictions were checked through primary PDFs/author pages rather than labeled broken links.

The [manifest](ROUND_MANIFEST.json) records model/effort requests, output hashes, source identity and scope checks. No engine change, numerical validation or performance result follows from these audit artifacts. The next deliverable would be a selected, corrected shared specification; this round stops at proposals and review as requested.
