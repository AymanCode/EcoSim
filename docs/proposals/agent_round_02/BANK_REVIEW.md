## Verdict and concrete bank rules

**BANK-P01 / C03 — version the claim before changing its rate.** For *new registered loans*, use a fixed annual nominal quote `a ≥ 0`, `r = a/52` per weekly tick, `N ≥ 1` installments, and payment `P r/[1-(1+r)^(-N)]` (or `P/N` at zero rate). Freeze rate, principal, unpaid interest due, `first_due_tick`, funder, subtype, borrower, ID and version. First due is **the tick after origination** for all new contracts; phase-6.8 medical debt is recorded immediately but skipped by that tick's 9.5 collector. At each due tick, charge contractual interest on outstanding *principal once*, apply actual payment to due interest then principal, and retain unpaid interest as a separate claim. No interest is charged **on** unpaid interest, and there is no extra penalty accrual. Fable's phrase “no accrual” must mean no delinquency capitalization/penalty for this candidate; literally never charging contractual interest on unpaid principal after a miss would be a different, interest-free forbearance arm. A payment below the scheduled due by a declared currency tolerance counts as a miss, including partial payment; a full installment resets the consecutive-miss streak. Partial cash still reduces the actual funder's claim once. The quoted installment count decreases only on a full payment, extending payoff after missed weeks; final payment is capped by outstanding principal plus unpaid interest. This calendar-extension choice is uncalibrated and must be compared with fixed-maturity/balloon treatment.

On a selected default, the lender's write-off is a **non-cash asset/loss entry**; borrower discharge is a separate, explicit simplifying event that closes the claim once, clears derived mirrors and bars later collection. A surviving legal claim would need a collector and exit priority. Fable FP07 confirms firms lack a live K-default and mortgages lack even a miss count. Household K=8 is the existing *zero-payment* default threshold; extending it to partial shortfalls is a new rule. For ordinary firms, K=8 is only a bounded candidate, to compare with K=4/12 and a funded/recorded short forbearance arm. Mortgage K is a separate Housing/Bank choice; first add miss telemetry without silently selecting default. Default never automatically exits a firm or grants immediate reborrowing: a cooldown, settled positive capacity and credit-history check gate reapplication. Treasury-funded principal and later receipts belong to treasury, including interest, with no bank servicing fee. Existing active ordinary loans retain frozen v1 full-term-fee schedules until payoff; existing mortgages retain their v1 PMT and phase-6.6b owner. New mortgages may share v2 *claim fields* but remain separately serviced. A migration needs an explicit claim conversion. Fallback: label the old `principal × (1+a)` as a full-term fee and exclude term/rate comparisons.

Housing's `LoanContract` remains a separate amortizing mortgage with one phase-6.6b collector, outside the ordinary phase-9.5 loop. Its direct reserve debit and principal join a **consolidated exposure view once**. Ordinary, medical, treasury-funded and mortgage IDs stay distinct across origination, mirrors, exit and telemetry. The no-bank unregistered medical fallback has unresolved funding under K02; disable it in a cash-closed pilot. At a quoted 10% annual, 26- and 104-week loans must have different total interest; these are uncalibrated terms.

**BANK-P02 / C01 — underwrite observable repayment and ration finite cash.** Build borrower dues once from unique active contract IDs. Household mortgages are excluded because the provider firm owes them; a firm's mortgage PMT enters its debt service exactly once. Household recurring capacity uses prior *settled net* wage and transfers (`last_wage_income + last_transfer_income + last_other_income`, where the last field holds negative assessed wage tax), less essential Food/rent and **existing installments once**. Proposed new PMT must fit a bounded fraction of that residual; deposits may support a one-time purchase but are not recurring income. Firms use trailing settled revenue **per tick**: `trailing_revenue_12t` is a 12-tick EMA, not a 12-week sum (`Economy._update_credit_scores`), less payroll, essential costs and existing contract PMTs. Keep purpose, score, leverage and housing DSCR/LTV gates. Alpha, expense proxies and windows are uncalibrated assumptions. No future wage, sale, transfer or tax receipt is spendable early. A cash-rich firm with no buyers fails purpose; sellable demand with inadequate cash fails liquidity. Credit cannot create doctors or housing units.

Bank cash after required reserves and a bounded withdrawal buffer limits ordinary lending. The **dynamic** buffer reads recent actual aggregate withdrawals, such as Fable's proposed four-tick window, with fixed initialization and capped history; it is not a static β amount or a deposit guarantee. `BankAgent.withdraw` returns actual cash and reduces reserves and household deposit liability equally. Existing phase order and stable ID order new applications; report denials by capacity, purpose, reserves or treasury. Deny an indivisible purpose unless the offer plus committed cash pays its full price. Fallback: payment-to-settled-income gate and existing reserve check with **no dynamic buffer**. Treasury emergency credit needs a separate prior-close cash-limited authorization; it never rescues deposits.

**C01 / HH-P02:** Household phase-2 planning can subtract weekly rent and **already registered** debt once from conservatively estimated liquid resources; the bank supplies an O(L) per-borrower due index, excluding provider-owned mortgages and same-tick future medical debt. Fable's `cash + 0.9·deposit − dues` is a **desired-demand estimate**, neither escrow nor a promise of withdrawal; a bank-wide availability factor would still be an estimate. The bank's underwriting uses the same existing installment sum **once**, not C01's already netted liquidity followed by another subtraction. After Household's R2-HH-BANK-01 objection, Bank conditionally accepts **actual matched essential Food first**, capped by unmet `min_food_per_tick`, stock, price and released cash. Unsupplied reservation releases before current rent/arrears at 6.5; then accepted funded care at 6.8, registered debt at 9.5 and discretionary goods from residual cash. This protects a cash-poor buyer's limited Food purchase but can raise arrears, lender misses and provider mortgage distress; a cash-rich buyer can pay all if stock exists. Scarce bank reserves can block Food funding. Goods quantity, seller receipt, inventory, subsidy/tax basis and household debit must settle atomically because today's resolver credits sales before phase-10 household debit (IR06). Until that coordinated revision, C01 and this order are planning/diagnostic only. Phase-9.5 debt cannot spend phase-10 wages/transfers or receive automatic credit/relief. Cash withdrawn for denied care stays household cash; no provider receipt/treatment occurs. Defer pre-rent critical-care escrow unless a qualified slot, quote, consent and cash are confirmed before 6.5.

**C09 / FP01:** Fable shows the early long-term capital loan actually removes reserves with **no recipient**, and its intended recycling counter is later reset. Bank and Firm conditionally prefer a route-tagged settled payment: bank reserve debit pays the implicit capital supplier directly on the firm’s behalf, then phase 8.5 distributes that exact amount equally to households once; reset only at tick start. Firm gets the asset and one contract/derived mirror, not freely spendable principal as well. Fable's proposed `_collect_misc_revenue` repair would instead skim a random tax and distribute to at most 50 selected households. It is a viable *alternative distribution institution*, not a neutral accounting correction; compare it separately if selected. For every ordinary, early, service-upgrade, mortgage, self-funded unit and public-project route, C09 must name payer, recipient, asset timing, mirror and recycle exactly once. No complete funding, no asset increment. FP03 also means housing expansion is inert in the standard factory, so a delayed-units question needs a live funded route first.

**C04 / FP09:** At firm exit, positive cash pays **actually accrued** worker/tax claims if present, then identified bank, treasury, direct-treasury and mortgage claims pari passu as unsecured claims when no collateral-sale proceeds exist; owners receive only positive residual. This is the conditional Bank/Firm/Housing compromise, not source behavior. Each funder recognizes its unpaid loss once; direct treasury loans and mortgages are currently dropped at exit. An unfinished unit opens none; paid proxy is sunk, unpaid earmarks release, and no automatic project refund claim appears. A firm with negative cash pays zero liquidation cash. Mandatory founder equity versus a zero-equity controller remains an institution choice; a new loan cannot count as founder cash twice.

**BANK-P03 — choose a labeled quote experiment.** Fable agrees this is deferred. After C03 and a declared price measure, a bounded exogenous funding quote changes **new** bank loans and, after a chosen lag, deposit quotes. Existing fixed contracts and treasury terms stay frozen; paid deposit interest needs actual bank earnings. The firm opportunity-cost hurdle can move even for self-financed firms (IR03), while no deposits or lendable cash blocks other channels. Observe applications, approvals, paid interest, output and a named price series. `target_inflation_rate` is no live controller. Fallback: a labeled quote stress test under the old full-term-fee convention, without annual-rate or inflation-policy claim. A central-bank institution is a later choice.

**C06/C07 and FP08:** Government and Bank conditionally agree that treasury rescue is **off** in the first cash-closed care pilot. A later emergency-credit arm draws only prior-close unencumbered cash from its own cap, not care/rent/project earmarks; an unused reservation stays restricted through expiry and returns at the next close. That can deny care while idle credit cash exists, a disclosed fiscal tradeoff. Bank's consolidated exposure distinguishes its own risk from treasury-serviced receivables. For C07, changing the benefit-linked private wage floor can alter *later settled* net wage and therefore underwriting, but a posted wage or phase-2 expectation is not recurring repayment cash. FP08 confirms housing pricing adds mortgage PMT twice and leaves a stale mirror after payoff. Mortgage underwriting and pricing must read one live contract-derived PMT and actual settled contract rent, never mirror plus PMT or a hoped-for vacant ask; renewal caps can thereby increase mortgage misses, rather than being neutral to the lender.
