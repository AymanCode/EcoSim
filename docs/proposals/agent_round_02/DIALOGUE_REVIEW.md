## Transmission and failure paths the final audit must retain

- **Tax and rebates:** ordinary tax changes expected net income and later settled cash; demand then affects seller receipts, hiring and investment. Treasury may use realized receipts for another destination. Authorization at close t followed by rebate at phase 10 of t+1 reaches the next normal phase-2 plan at **t+2**, possibly later under declared cache cadence. Debt or rent may absorb it; an empty treasury or empty shelf blocks the intended purchase channel.
- **Housing protection:** a renewal cap or cure period can preserve an incumbent tenancy; delayed/lower provider cash may instead cause mortgage misses, reduce future supply or trigger exit. Entrants face the vacancy ask and finite units. Essential Food priority intensifies this tradeoff. No relief envelope means no public rescue.
- **Public care:** funded low-cash patients may complete more visits; treasury loses cash available for relief, rebates or projects, while providers still owe payroll when funding ends. A scarce qualified roster can block extra completions. Later health changes future need and labor availability, so matched starting episodes do not mean identical illness histories forever.
- **Credit and construction:** a fully funded project creates a debt claim now and usable capacity later; provider debt service can precede rent from new units. Denial, no workers/buyers, default or pre-completion exit can prevent the benefit. A rate quote can affect both new debt and the return hurdle for a self-funded firm; it is not yet an inflation-targeting institution.
- **Public Services capacity:** an assigned worker-week reduces current private output while consuming payroll and treasury cash. A future slot is useless without a later worker/customer and creates no doctor. Its implicit technology and tax timing must remain disclosed abstractions.
