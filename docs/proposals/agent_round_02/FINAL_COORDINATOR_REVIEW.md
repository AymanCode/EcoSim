## Corrections a future specification must carry forward

### FC01 — Define scheduled dues by contract, and separate planning from settlement

Fable C01 sums each household loan's nominal `payment_per_tick`. C03 simultaneously introduces contract versions, first due ticks, unpaid interest, partial payments and capped final installments. Those interfaces need **one contract-derived amount scheduled for the current tick**, keyed by unique claim ID and version: skip not-yet-due/deferred claims, exclude provider mortgages, and cap a final due at the remaining live claim. The planning index and collector must read the same pre-cash due; actual payment is then limited by actual funds. Current collection already caps payment by scheduled amount, remaining balance and cash ([collector](../../../backend/economy.py#L6511)). A sum of all nominal PMTs is only a legacy proxy.

The pre-enqueue view can inform Care at entry and Household planning later, but education spending happens between those readers ([entry](../../../backend/economy.py#L1529), [education](../../../backend/economy.py#L1693)). Use the indexed dues again with **then-current cash** at the phase-2 plan; this is bounded arithmetic, not another loan scan or knowledge of future income. If selected, its residual-liquidity value must consistently feed both the planner's drawdown and its final liquidity cap ([planner](../../../backend/economy.py#L851)). It remains an estimate of desired spending: later withdrawals, rent, care and debt require actual funds. The simpler fallback is diagnostic-only, without a protected-Food or solvency claim.

### FC02 — The net-income fallback needs a valid stored observation

Fable correctly uses the ordinary wage/tax locals and a finite [0,1] belief bound. Its alternative “last settled net ordinary income, zero new state” is not available from the current persistent fields: `last_wage_income` includes CEO salary, while `last_other_income` is negative wage tax ([settlement](../../../backend/economy.py#L1193)). Store **one ordinary-net-income value** if choosing that fallback, or explicitly choose CEO-inclusive income as a different signal. Do not report zero added state for an unavailable observation.

For the smallest belief design, update after phase 10 and read at the next normal plan, subject to the stated cache refresh. A “lag 0” arm requires a separately defined pre-plan announcement update; it cannot obtain the current tick's later tax assessment early. The coordinator's earlier 0.615 example used the fallback p90 multiplier 1.2; the default initializer instead fills the scalers using a fixed seeded draw ([initializer](../../../backend/agents.py#L6429), [top-band reader](../../../backend/agents.py#L6918)). The source's configured range and actual stored scaler, rather than that example, justify avoiding a 0.6 belief cap. Progressive brackets remain intact.

### FC03 — Recover cash first, then write off the correct residual claim

Legacy bank dictionaries, housing `LoanContract` objects and direct treasury fields are different representations ([legacy write-off](../../../backend/agents.py#L6054), [firm exit](../../../backend/economy.py#L4510)). A new v2 claim is outstanding principal plus interest actually due, **excluding unearned future interest**; the legacy full-term fee remains a versioned convention. Do not pass every representation into the old bank write-off method or copy its `remaining` semantics into v2.

For an exit waterfall, collect available positive cash once, credit each actual funder once, reduce its claim, and recognize only the unpaid residual as a non-cash loss. Borrower discharge is a separate selected event. Fable P0's missing-claim write-off repair must not silently pre-empt P4's recovery order: absent a selected recovery institution, specify claim registration and loss telemetry first. Accrued worker/tax priority applies only to recorded liabilities; current phase-5 wages are already debited and cannot be billed again. This preserves the roles' distinction between bookkeeping loss, cash recovery and legal forgiveness.

### FC04 — Consume the named project worker's labor once

Fable C09 proposes decrementing the Services worker count at [the phase-5 clamp](../../../backend/agents.py#L5248). However, [experience-adjusted production](../../../backend/economy.py#L4435) has already summed individual workers' skill, health and morale. Lowering the later count alone can leave the assigned worker's productive contribution in the earlier result or have no effect when another limit binds.

The minimum project specification must name the assigned incremental worker and exclude that ID from the productive roster **before** individual capacity is calculated, using the same available roster for the later clamp. Retain the worker in employment/payroll and debit the existing wage bill once. Actual Services supply then reads the resulting `last_units_produced` ([market supply](../../../backend/economy.py#L3707)). Test that the worker cannot serve both uses; do not require output to fall by a fixed amount in every case, because demand, planned output or available slots can already bind. The later slot, contract receipt and tax record remain separately tracked.

### FC05 — Keep renewal caps, asking-price timing and maintenance separate

Fable's phase-9 ask recommendation is a workable **candidate**, with Firm's phase-6.5 alternative still unaccepted. Its claim of identical information at both times is too broad: the insertion point relative to eviction matters, and staffing/debt may have changed since the phase-1 plan. Specify the observed roster, claims and occupancy, not just the commit phase. In particular, phase-1 computation committed at 9 is not a fresh phase-9 computation.

Removing the housing return from [the generic price-cap helper](../../../backend/economy.py#L296) does **not** implement renewal rent stabilization: the helper reads generic price targets/levels, and [the current schema](../../../backend/policy_schema.py#L38) does not allow Housing as that target. Broadening it would be a new asking-price policy with a different multiplier. Keep that bypass for the first renewal-only slice and apply the selected rent lever at the contract-renewal owner; vacancy asks remain uncapped in that slice. An ask-cap arm must have its own declared rule.

Fable also recommends replacing the repair-cost base with mean contract rent. Current [repairs](../../../backend/economy.py#L6004) depend on asking price and units, and their cash is redistributed. A capped contract-rent base would lower modeled maintenance costs and recipient cash as well as rent receipts, and a zero-tenant mean is undefined. Preserve the common committed-ask repair rule in the first isolated renewal comparison; a maintenance-rule change is a separate institution with an explicit empty-provider case. Otherwise the rent-cap experiment would also introduce a lower-cost assumption.

### FC06 — Preserve nonoverlapping fiscal claims and explicit evidence limits

The Government review excludes any payable already covered by an earmark when calculating free cash. Carry that qualifier into Fable C06's shorter formula: **an encumbered payable is reserved once**, and a restricted sub-balance is not subtracted again from an already net spendable balance. State whether the recorded treasury balance is total or unrestricted cash. Releasing an unspent restriction creates no new money. Eligibility categories for the receipt fraction must also be declared: the Government candidate names wage/profit/price-ceiling/property receipts; Fable adds investment-tax receipts. That is a selectable fiscal profile, not an already agreed category list.

The finite care envelope is free **for funded visits**, not an uncapped entitlement. Default baseline treasury gaps, old spending routes, fixed-tariff/fixed-roster restrictions and unavailable credit must remain visible in comparison results. The evidence supports proposed mechanisms only. Numeric choices such as care slope, cure duration, lease term, construction cost and default threshold remain hypotheses for later sensitivity checks.

## How to use the proposed package order

Keep Fable's separation of source repairs from behavioral/institutional extensions, with these qualifications:

1. **Prepare accounting specifications first:** capital payment recipient, claim identity and mirrors, tax-snapshot completeness, qualified care capacity and source-route inventory. Several repairs change flows; they are not guaranteed to reproduce old outcomes. Adding cash fields to an existing bracket reader restores its current rule, while redesigning those brackets is separate and not a priority here.
2. **Version shared contracts before dependent implementation:** dues and due dates, entry observations, ask/contract fields, fiscal restrictions, episode lifecycle, exit claims and assigned project labor. Rules v1.1 remain unchanged in this round; the proposed v1.2 has not been adopted.
3. **Specify small comparisons separately:** take-home planning, the benefit-floor switch, one funded fiscal destination, fixed-tariff care, renewal/arrears and funded entry. State each comparison's constants and affected counterparts. Technical role acceptance does not select the policy arm.
4. **Keep larger extensions bounded:** delayed Housing needs one reachable funded route and an explicit disposition for the fourth free route; Services projects need FC04; atomic goods settlement is a coordinated market change; training, new triage, monetary institutions and government-system comparisons require their own resource and governance rules. Their minimal diagnostics/fallbacks remain useful now.

The field counts in Fable C10 are provisional inventories, not hard caps or measurements: choosing an ordinary-net observation, named worker assignment, notice deadline or distinct claim state can require additional fields. Accept the bounded indexed complexity objective, then measure the selected integrated package. C10 is a common written constraint, not evidence of a negotiated six-role vote; all acceptance claims must be read at the message/condition level in the [dialogue record](DIALOGUE_REVIEW.md).
