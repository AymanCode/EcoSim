## Verdict and concrete firm rules

**FIRM-P01 / IR02–IR04: gate ordinary capital by *incremental sellable contribution*, after repairing the payment route.** At phase 1, the ordinary one-unit planner may read only its existing lagged expected sales, bounded lost-sales signal, opening inventory, posted price, known variable unit cost, planned staffing, capacity and debt schedule. For storable Food, first use available inventory against expected sales; for flow Services, do not carry last week's unsold output as stock. Estimate extra weekly units as the minimum of the capital unit's physical increment and the positive expected-sales shortfall against feasible output with current capital and planned labor. Multiply by nonnegative price less incremental operating cost, then compare with depreciation plus the existing lending-rate opportunity-cost proxy. This is a return hurdle; **principal repayment belongs in a separate liquidity/underwriting hurdle**, not in operating expense. Keep the current one-unit limit and 10% smoothed-margin/survival gates initially. Cash funding must leave the existing eight-week payroll buffer; a requested loan adds no asset or expenditure unless the actual funder approves and disburses. No worker or no expected buyer means no investment even if the posted price is high. A lower rate can still change a cash-rich firm's opportunity-cost hurdle, although it has no new-loan installment. The minimal fallback is merely a positive expected-sales gap gate, retaining the current value-of-MPK calculation if contribution estimates prove noisy. Neither version claims that sales expectation observes all rejected price-sensitive demand.

Before selecting that behavior, resolve the source-backed **IR04/FP01 reset and missing-recipient hazard**. `Economy.step` calls early long-term Services/Housing expansion before `FirmAgent.plan_capital_investment`; the former adds `capital_investment_this_tick`, and the latter resets it to zero. The bank has debited reserves, but neither firm cash nor phase-8.5 households receive the principal, and the firm's debt mirrors omit it. Replace this overloaded counter conceptually with one tick-scoped, route-tagged *settled capital payment* amount in model currency, reset once at tick start, appended only after actual firm cash debit or bank/treasury disbursement and never by a later planner. The phase-8.5 household credit must equal that amount exactly once. For the early bank loan, the bank reserve debit pays the implicit capital supplier directly on the firm's behalf, with a matching firm loan claim and derived debt-service view; it must not also become freely spendable firm cash. This preserves the intended equal-to-all-households recipient. Fable's proposed reroute through `_collect_misc_revenue` would instead skim a random tax and pay at most 50 selected households, so it is a separate distribution choice, not the smallest accounting repair. The ordinary cash/loan capital route, early long-term service/housing route, phase-6 service slot upgrade through the miscellaneous pool, self-funded housing construction, and housing mortgage need separate route-to-asset/payment entries. A denied loan changes none of them. Delayed housing capacity needs HOUSE-P03's cancellation and creditor rule.

**FIRM-P02 / K02 / IR11: entry is a funded transfer, ownership is a residual claim, exit is one settlement.** At phase 13, choose a founder household by a stable ID/tick keyed procedure from those with *actual post-settlement cash* above a bounded next-week essential-Food allowance plus known rent and registered dues. Debit its actual contribution and credit the entrant once; record that household in `owners`, separate from `ceo_household_id`. Reject an attempted bootstrap if no one can fund its minimum, and permit a failed bank/treasury route to fall back only to an actual contribution. A loan-funded entrant receives only settled principal from its named funder, with one contract ID and an owner/controller explicitly selected even if the owner has zero paid equity. My preferred first slice permits zero-equity control, labels it as an assumption, and prevents dividend distributions until due debt and an operating cash buffer are covered. Mandatory founder equity is an alternative institutional choice, not an accounting necessity; requiring it would ration entry by household wealth and should be compared openly. Existing factory endowments remain labeled initial conditions, not retroactive household debits. Household contribution consumes current cash that might otherwise buy Food, rent or care; an equity claim is not a spendable deposit or a guaranteed dividend.

At phase 12 exit, close employment and only *identified, actually accrued* claims. Pay available positive firm cash to accrued unpaid payroll/tax claims under an explicit priority, then divide the remainder pro rata across unsecured registered bank, treasury, housing-mortgage and direct-treasury claims, recording each partial collection once. With no modeled collateral sale, no lender gets an invented secured recovery. Write off unpaid balances against their actual funder; a write-off is not another cash debit. Pay owners only a positive cash residual. Unsold goods, capital and unfinished projects have zero automatic cash recovery in the minimal rule. Tenant arrears are a provider receivable paired to a tenant liability, not a cash asset or new treasury payable; provider exit needs cancellation or an explicit assignee. An unspent project earmark returns to treasury availability, while a paid installment is sunk unless a distinct enforceable refund contract was chosen. No project refund/payable repeats by default. A firm with negative cash has no liquidation cash to distribute. Bank/Housing/Government must agree on priority and contract identity. The fallback closes entry to unfunded entrants and limits exit to scenarios without new claims; it cannot certify creditor accounting.

**FIRM-P03 / HH-P03 / FP05: make the imposed multiplier an explicit switch first.** The current `1.5 × unemployment_benefit` floor binds private Food offers under high unemployment, but generic Services returns early with its legal-minimum path; the multiplier reaches Services only through the reservation-blocked raise. My original description overstated the normal Services scope. Adopt C07's `benefit_wage_floor_multiplier`, default **1.5** for baseline replay and **0** for a declared no-mechanical-floor arm, at both actual readers. Keep the legal minimum, existing rejection response, revenue/debt affordability and per-tick bounds. This is an institution comparison, not a new worker rule. Phase-1 offers remain gross. HH-P03's household net-gain behavior is a **separate diagnostic/later rule** using lagged withholding and announced benefit, not assumed active with the switch; actual wages/taxes arrive later. No vacancy, ineligible worker, unaffordable offer or unfunded benefit can block the later behavioral channel. Baseline, Housing and Healthcare labor paths remain separately specified. If the switch is not selected, retain and label the 1.5 link as part of the scenario.

## Fable 5.1 disposition and later counterparty responses

**FIRM-P01, C09, FP01.** I accept Fable's stronger source finding that early bank principal currently reaches no recipient and that debt mirrors omit the loan. I reject its proposed `_collect_misc_revenue` route as the *default repair*: unlike existing phase-8.5 capital recycling, it taxes a random share and directs the balance to a small beneficiary set. Keep one direct implicit-supplier disbursement and equal-household credit, plus one firm loan claim and derived debt-service mirror, with capacity only after full payment. A misc-pool route is viable as an explicitly compared distribution institution. For ordinary Food, the gap uses storable opening inventory plus feasible staffed output; for Services, use own bounded lost sales and sustained sell-through/full-utilization evidence, not a carried stock count or a single high posted price. Fable's positive-gap-only rule is the fallback; contribution net of variable cost is a separately selected behavioral extension. A one-tick household tax-information lag (C02) compounds existing sales-expectation smoothing, which may delay investment; it does not give the firm advance knowledge. C01's rent/debt planning reserve may reduce later customer demand, but without atomic household debit and seller settlement it remains a diagnostic, not guaranteed cash.

**FIRM-P02, C03/C04, FP07/09.** I accept one versioned contract ID per new seed loan, derived firm debt mirrors, annual-to-weekly amortization only for new contracts, and legacy fee schedules until payoff.
