## Verdict and candidate rules

**GOV-P01 is necessary before attributing a tax result to its use of revenue.** The live phase-10 household update and phase-11 treasury recognition follow care, rent and goods clearing. Existing baseline unemployment transfers can push signed treasury cash negative; subsidies, infrastructure and social spending have different cash gates. A surplus “bond purchase” routes cash to a miscellaneous beneficiary pool without a modeled bond claim. Thus the comparison must report actual collected tax, baseline transfers, paid subsidies, old discretionary outlays, pool routing and closing treasury cash. A reserve choice does not hold these other cash-sensitive outlays fixed (IR07–08). The firm-tax snapshot mismatch identified in Round 01 is a separate source correction, not a reason to count assessed tax as collected revenue.

My **candidate cash-funded closure**, for one declared scenario arm, is:

1. At each completed tick record `eligible_receipts_t` as actual wage, profit/price-ceiling and property-tax credits in currency, separately by source. It is a realized flow, never a computed “extra tax” relative to an unavailable online counterfactual. Record `treasury_close_t` as signed currency after existing transfers and baseline discretionary/pool routing. Record existing program disbursements once, not as fresh deductions from the closing balance.
2. Maintain `earmarked_t` as treasury cash reserved for named next-tick programs, and `recognized_payable_t` only where an identified counterparty has an enforceable due claim. Excluding any payable already counted in an earmark, `free_t = max(0, treasury_close_t - earmarked_t - payable_reserve_t)`. Earmarking is neither a new asset nor public debt. All cash guards must read the same free-cash view; otherwise the same balance can fund a clinic visit, rental relief and project. Reject nonfinite or negative appropriations.
3. A fixed scenario rule chooses a fraction `u` in `[0,1]`, then creates at most `min(u × eligible_receipts_t, free_t)` of new next-tick authorization. Its source is **positive residual treasury cash at the prior close**, left after that tick's baseline transfers and existing discretionary/pool route; receipts limit new allocation but are not a second cash stock. The reserve comparator leaves the same amount unearmarked. Predeclare nonoverlapping destination caps/shares for care, rent relief, emergency credit, a project and rebates. The recommended first comparison selects **one destination at a time** against reserve. In a mixed arm, recognized payables take priority over new authorization; fixed opening shares, not arrival order, divide care/relief/credit, with any project/rebate residual declared in advance. That is a candidate policy preference, not a source fact or user decision. Denied claims cannot raid another envelope.

This closure does **not** impose a hard balanced-budget rule on existing baseline unemployment transfers or retroactively cancel existing subsidies. At authorization, transfer actual cash internally into restricted program sub-balances: total treasury money is unchanged, ordinary spendable cash falls by the same amount, and old cash guards must respect the restriction. A later soft baseline transfer debits the ordinary balance and may leave a signed financing gap; it cannot spend restricted funds. This continues the current signed-cash convention without asserting a lender or bond. If there is no positive free close, make no new appropriation. At a published expiry, unused program cash returns to free treasury at fiscal close, never as an opportunistic same-tick rescue. A debt-financed entitlement is an alternative requiring W14's lender, liability, rate and repayment. The fallback is reserve-only with the old automatic spending route retained in both arms or separately replaced and labeled.

**Timing and competing claims.** Coverage for a phase-6.8 visit or phase-6.5 rent relief uses its *prior-close* restricted cash, never current-tick tax forecasts. A covered visit is free only after a qualified slot and fixed full-price reservation; completion debits its envelope and credits the provider once. Failed delivery releases the reservation to the same envelope; exhaustion means funding denial or explicit self-pay consent, not surprise debt or endless free care. A due episode may persist outside the physical queue for retry. Rent relief, off by default, pays a named current shortfall directly to the landlord before verified old arrears, reducing the paired tenant claim once and arriving before phase-6.6b mortgage service. It can avert notice or free provider cash for debt service but is no mortgage guarantee. Use a bounded, declared eligibility rule (for example active tenancy, verified shortfall and a fixed pre-policy low-cash test); its threshold and cap need selection. A rebate authorized at close is tagged apart from unemployment benefit, paid at phase 10 next tick from its envelope and seen in the following consumption plan/cache refresh. An authorization alone is not household income.

**GOV-P02 should name a small deliverable rather than call any outlay “investment.”** My narrow candidate is one extra **Services worker slot** (`production_capacity_units`, interpreted as an integer slot in the live Services path) on one existing provider, activated no earlier than the next tick after work. One bounded project ID records provider, authorized ceiling/remaining earmark in currency, one required worker-week, start/expiry tick, paid installments, completed slots and cancellation reason. The actual scarce input is a retained incremental Services worker-week assigned after labor matching and subtracted from that firm's ordinary phase-5 service production. The firm pays that week's wage from its cash or separately authorized working capital. At institutional close, treasury rechecks reserved cash and pays completed work once as provider contract revenue; the tax treatment must recognize this phase-11.5 receipt on the next tax snapshot, not retroactively as phase-9 sales. The installment never also enters the miscellaneous pool or capital-recycling counter. A newly opened slot is useful only if the firm retains enough workers and customers to use it; it creates no doctor, clinic room, visit, housing unit or global productivity gain. This is a staffing/capacity-upgrade proxy, not physical construction. The existing immediate infrastructure multiplier is a distinct, labeled comparison arm.

Authorization encumbers real cash but creates no output or third-party claim. Before worker assignment, reserve a full quoted installment in the restricted project envelope. Completed work creates one provider payable, settled from that reserved cash at phase 11.5; if a fault prevents payment, retain and report the payable rather than pretending completed work vanished. No payment means no new slot. No suitable provider, incremental worker, paid payroll or reserved cash means no start. If the firm exits before completion, release **unpaid and unearned** earmark, cancel the slot and show paid installments as sunk cost. No automatic refund receivable exists; adding one would require FIRM-P02's creditor rule. At short expiry unused cash returns at fiscal close. A completed slot persists unless a separately funded maintenance/depreciation rule is selected. Simpler fallback: buy one actually produced Services unit for a named recipient, without claiming a durable asset.

**Deferred contract receipt:** Phase-11.5 cash paid to the contractor is stored by project and firm ID as `contract_income_pending_tax`. At the next phase-7 firm tax snapshot, include that gross deferred receipt once; the project worker's wage was already in the earlier week's wage bill and cannot be deducted again. At phase 9, show the receipt in period revenue/profit without crediting cash again. The live `apply_sales_and_profit` overwrites `last_revenue`/`last_profit` with current sales, so a separate pending-income consumer must survive that overwrite and clear only after tax and book recognition. If the firm exits first, assess the pending receipt before owner residual. This preserves a receipt, while the current no-loss-carry tax convention can still misalign wage deduction and later income; a matched cost carry would be a separate tax-rule change. This is a requested interface, not existing behavior.

## Response to Fable 5.1 initial audit

**C06/GOV-P01/FP10/FP18:** I accept one treasury spendable-cash register and Fable's finding that default social spending, surplus-to-misc routing, subsidies, bailouts, public works and treasury-funded loans compete. Each route needs the same restricted-cash guard and actual paid/due/authorized telemetry. Fable's GOV-P01 card defines `eligible = receipts − existing_committed_outlays`, while its rebate is then capped by `spendable_cash`. That risks deducting an already paid outlay twice if spendable cash is measured at close. My narrower convention is `eligible_receipts = actual tax credits` (flow) and `free_close = max(0, closing signed cash − nonoverlapping carried restrictions/payable reserve)` (stock); new authorization is `min(u × eligible_receipts, free_close)`. Existing paid programs are included in closing cash exactly once. If a future rule defines incremental *net* revenue instead, it needs its own counterfactual and cannot also subtract the same commitments from closing cash. Existing sector cost-share must be disabled for the **same covered visit** in the new full-payer arm, or two public paths may pay it. A zero-care envelope never guarantees a free visit.

Fable recommends a pre-plan next-tick rebate so that household consumption can respond immediately. The Household reviewer and I conditionally chose a **phase-10 tagged payment** for the first slice: household cash rises once from restricted treasury cash, but phase-2 demand responds at the following tick or declared performance-cache refresh. Pre-plan payment is a separate timing arm that needs an earlier debit/household credit and cache invalidation; do not blend its faster effect into the same rebate comparison. **C02:** headline tax and coverage rules can be announced at a validated safe boundary; neither the realized wage-tax percentile nor remaining care cash is promised to the household. A one-tick tax-belief lag is a candidate, not an observed expectation parameter. A rebate rule may be announced, but an individual amount is not spendable or certain before payment.

Fable's C06 priority list puts care, relief, projects and rebates in a fixed order. I prefer **predeclared nonoverlapping shares per scenario** after actual recognized payables and existing program treatment, because that exposes the normative tradeoff without an accidental phase-order race. A care-first or relief-first ordering can be a separately named institution. Neither is approved by the user. When old baseline transfers push the ordinary treasury sub-balance negative, report the signed gap and keep previously restricted cash tied to its original program; this protection can deepen the unrestricted gap and alter later budget pressure, so it is a declared intervention, not a neutral accounting fix or a new bond. If that protection is rejected, revert to moment-of-service cash caps and disclose that an earlier authorization was not guaranteed. Fable's C01 planning-only obligations view can inform desired goods demand, but it does not enforce Household/Housing's proposed actual Food → rent → care → debt priority. That stronger institution needs atomic seller receipt, inventory and household debit settlement; until then, the priority remains diagnostic.

**C04/C09/GOV-P02/FP01:** I conditionally accept cash-only exit recovery: accrued worker/tax claims first, then positive residual cash pro rata among identified bank, mortgage and direct-treasury creditors, owners last, with actual-funder write-offs. The order and treatment of tax claims still require the shared exit contract; no claim may be paid twice. An unfinished project adds no slot and no automatic government refund claim. Fable's “refund unspent encumbrance” means **release the internal restriction** on cash still in treasury, not credit treasury again. Earned but unpaid contractor work is a payable and cannot be released as unused cash. C09 should record public-project payment directly to the named Services contractor and one delayed slot increment; routing that same payment through miscellaneous beneficiaries would duplicate or divert the contractor receipt. Our assigned and consumed worker-week is stronger than Fable's “provider has ≥1 employee” gate: an ordinary worker cannot produce full private services and install capacity for free in the same week.

FP01 identifies a bank-principal debit with no surviving recipient in the early long-term capital route. Correct that missing counterparty before any project reuses the route, but **miscellaneous routing is not a neutral repair**. The source intended `capital_investment_this_tick` to pay every household equally at phase 8.5; Fable's proposed misc route instead applies a random tax skim and pays at most 50 selected beneficiaries later. My minimum source repair preserves the stated distribution: one bank-reserve debit disburses the loan to an abstract capital supplier on the firm's behalf, a settled route-tagged payment survives the later planner reset, phase 8.5 credits all households once, and the firm receives the loan liability plus one capacity increment. A paired firm credit/debit is equivalent only if the firm cannot spend that loan cash twice. If misc beneficiaries are preferred, label a separate distribution choice and compare its tax/inequality effects. GOV-P02's direct named-contractor payment is a different route, with its asymmetry disclosed.
