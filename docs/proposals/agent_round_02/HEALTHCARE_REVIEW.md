## Verdict and smallest candidate

**CARE-P01: separate illness from the decision to seek care.** `HouseholdAgent.should_request_healthcare_service` currently creates an episode and consumes a pending visit without reading price. Retain one active due episode with stable ID and due tick; route with `min(255, current_tick − due_tick)` weeks of unmet age and report longer waits as `255+`. At entry, the household sees health, cash, estimated deposit access, debt, last posted price and *announced* coverage, but not later firm pricing, bank release or remaining public funds. Expected charge is last posted price in patient-pay and zero **conditional on a funded covered visit**; a public-cap miss produces funding wait, not predicted self-pay. A critical episode triggers a request; a noncritical one uses a bounded probability falling with expected charge relative to accessible resources. Reuse `price_sensitivity` only if its cross-sector meaning is accepted; otherwise use a common slope and existing resources. Draw with a separate keyed salt so same-state episode incidence stays fixed. Deferral or funding denial retries the same ID at the next eligible tick with no new illness draw or heal; no time-only expiry. Close on treatment or household exit; defer a payer-blind recovery rule until its threshold is chosen. Later treatment can change health and future need (IR10).

Candidate uncalibrated slope is `s = 0.5`, with 0 and 1 sensitivity arms. Define `r = 0` when expected patient charge is zero, including zero liquidity; otherwise `r = min(1, charge / max(planning_liquidity, 1 model currency))`, and `p = clamp(1 − s·r, 0, 1)`. Thus zero-charge, zero-liquidity patients can request; positive charge with no liquidity gives `p = 1 − s`, preserving a credit pathway when `s < 1`. C01 must build the read-only obligations/liquidity view **before** the entry request pass; Fable places it after enqueueing, too late for CARE-P01. This view informs demand, not bank release. At full capacity, extra requests cannot add visits. Fallback: price-blind requests and payment-denial comparison. Count requests, deferrals, funding refusals, physical waits, affordability removals and completions separately.

**CARE-P02: one named payer for one delivered visit.** Recommend a bounded public-purchasing comparison for the same basic visit and unchanged posted firm price. At the *prior* fiscal close, government earmarks actual uncommitted cash in a care envelope. At phase 6.8, the care resolver checks a qualified slot, fixes the quote, then reserves the whole price from the envelope. Only a completed visit debits treasury, credits `per_firm_sales` once for the full price, and heals once; an unsuccessful reservation is released. No future phase-10/11 tax receipt pays an earlier visit. A funded covered visit charges the patient zero. If the envelope is empty, record a funding denial or offer an explicit patient-paid visit before any withdrawal/loan; there is no surprise residual bill or debt. The patient-paid arm uses cash, actual bank-released deposit withdrawals, then at most one registered actual-funder medical loan for the full remaining amount. Failure to assemble the full price leaves the visit incomplete and provider receipt zero. An unsuccessful withdrawal remains a paired conversion from deposit to household cash, not care spending. The no-bank, unregistered fallback has an unspecified creditor and should be excluded from a cash-closed comparison. Preserve bank-funded registered credit in the patient-pay baseline; a treasury-backed medical loan is a separately authorized sensitivity case drawing only unencumbered cash, not the care/rent envelopes. Disabling all credit in both arms is a narrower, differently labeled comparison.

This is **free for funded visits**, not an uncapped entitlement. A guarantee beyond cash exhaustion needs a lender, liability and repayment. Separate opening care/rent/project envelopes prevent repeated use of treasury cash; zero care funding can leave staff idle and payroll due. Realized visit revenue enters ordinary firm profit/tax. Base wages remain firm obligations; the later healthcare-worker bonus draws from firm cash. The existing sector subsidy and configured visit-share paths must be **disabled for the same covered visit**, and set to zero for a full patient-pay comparison, or two public paths could pay one bill. Their current capped cost-share behavior is a labeled fallback, not the full-payer arm (FP18).

**CARE-P03: count people who can actually deliver visits.** `_healthcare_effective_capacity` can turn zero qualified capacity or unknown roster IDs into nominal capacity. Sum employed doctors/residents' `medical_visit_capacity`; students, unqualified workers and unknown IDs contribute zero. Preserve fractional carryover and allow only explicit test scaffolding. Hold the current sick-doctor priority common across payer arms and report displacement; health bands, wait-age ties and anti-starvation rules are separate choices. **Defer endogenous training** until seats, displaced labor, tuition/wage payer, debt creditor, placement and delay are specified. A Services project slot creates no doctor.
