## Verdict and smallest candidate rules

**HH-P01 — take-home expectation: conditionally viable.** At phase 2, estimate ordinary wage-tax share from the last settled *ordinary wage and assessed tax*, initialized for new workers from the announced headline rate and bounded to a valid fraction. An announcement can update the prior at the next safe boundary, but phase-7 population percentile and realized phase-10 assessment are unknowable at planning. Substitute estimated **net** wage for the existing wage-funded desired-spend term in the live batch planner; scalar parity is API hygiene, since Fable FP11 found that `HouseholdAgent.plan_consumption` has no live tick caller. Preserve existing MPC and individual traits. The estimate is model currency/week, **not cash**. No forecast wage, phase-2a loan, rebate, future dividend or subsidy increases the payment cap. Current source uses gross wage in `_batch_plan_consumption`; tax assessment and household debit are later (`Economy._build_household_tax_snapshots`, `GovernmentAgent.plan_taxes`, `_batch_apply_household_updates`). A cash-poor worker may change desired spend without changing purchases; a liquid worker may change orders if stock exists. Fallback: last settled net ordinary income, with a one-tick recognition lag and no new persistent field. Announcement response is a **chosen** assumption, not calibrated tax knowledge.

**HH-P02 — obligations and collectible payment: viable only with a shared resolver contract.** Before phase-2 goods planning, form one per-household due view in currency/week: active **weekly-collected** contract rent (`monthly_rent` in the source, despite its name) plus scheduled amounts on registered household loans, including a registered medical loan once. Subtract due amounts once from opening cash plus a conservative estimate of releasable deposits when capping discretionary demand, never from future wages. This is a planning reserve, not escrow, payment or proof of solvency. At phase 5b/6.5/6.8/9.5, a bank withdrawal returns only actual released cash and reduces the paired bank deposit liability; provider or lender receives only what was paid. Rent due and old arrears must be separate fields/claims; an unpaid amount is never landlord revenue. Confirmed care patient share can be considered only after the care owner defines an accepted, priced visit and funding status; speculative requests are not commitments. The current goods resolver records allocation/firm sale before the household phase-10 debit, so planning subtraction alone does **not** prevent an overdraw or reconcile a short-paid purchase (IR06). Recommend one shared payment-capacity ledger or reservation at allocation, with a declared priority and atomic reduction of goods quantity, seller revenue, subsidy, inventory and household debit when collectible funds run out. The conservative fallback is a diagnostic-only obligation-pressure measure until this contract is selected. The current planner counts cash plus 90% of deposits, while deposits can be inaccessible and rent, care and loans are later specialized settlements (`Economy._withdraw_deposits_for_planned_consumption`, `_clear_housing_rental_market`, `_collect_bank_loan_repayments`). The former is nominal access, not a guaranteed bank release.

**HH-P03 — net job gain: defer behavior until the joint labor interface is fixed.** Firms still post **gross currency/week** offers and pay matched gross wages. At a visible offer, a diagnostic may compare estimated after-tax offer pay to current net pay or announced baseline benefit; actual matching keeps the existing gross reservation. Preserve cash-stress, duration, health, skill, cooldown, medical-worker and vacancy gates. A cash-poor worker may accept a small gain; a cash-rich eligible worker may wait; no vacancy means no hire. Current `HouseholdAgent.plan_labor_supply` and `Economy._run_labor_matching` use gross signals. Fable C07 correctly separates a replay-preserving `benefit_wage_floor_multiplier` switch (1.5 baseline, zero arm) from the unselected household behavior. Fable's later gross-threshold formula is **not ready**: the existing `min_job_premium_over_unemployment = 1.05` is a *dimensionless multiplier*, not currency to add to benefit, and `max(existing_reservation, ...)` can erase the current desperation-driven downward reservation. Retain observed-offer net diagnostics before choosing any bounded behavioral rule.

The rules use existing traits and changing cash, debt, tenure and employment. At most one bounded tax belief per household is new durable state; aggregate loan dues once over active contracts. Scalar/batch and performance-cache paths must agree on when announcements, rent revisions, debt payoff and rebates become visible. No runtime result is claimed; the cumulative 5% median/p95 tick-time target covers the whole selected package.

## Reciprocal viability and payment-order objection

**R2-HH-BANK-01 — response to R2-BANK-HH-01.** The bank's once-per-borrower registered due view and actual-withdrawal-only promise are viable for HH-P02; they prevent nominal deposits and double-counted medical installments from inflating household purchasing power. For a cash-rich borrower, due subtraction leaves discretionary capacity; for a cash-poor tenant with no released deposit, it can drive that capacity to zero while arrears or missed debt remain visible. The bank's proposed rent → care → debt → goods priority would make all goods residual, including food. This conflicts with the existing `min_food_per_tick` need reader (`HouseholdAgent`, `Economy._batch_update_wellbeing`) and can deepen food shortfall even when a small purchase was physically available. A public care entitlement cannot be financed from the household's expected wage. Minimum compromise: consider a *bounded* essential-food claim from actual cash before current rent, then accepted patient-paid care, registered debt and discretionary goods; no bank guarantee of the claim, no purchase without supplier stock. A debt miss remains a lender claim/default path, not a hidden subsidy. If this ordering is rejected, disclose food deprivation and compare that institution as an explicit scenario rather than treating it as a neutral accounting fix.

**Source-order obstacle shared with Housing/Care/Bank (IR06).** `_withdraw_deposits_for_planned_consumption` moves only actual bank-released cash at phase 5b. `_clear_goods_market` at phase 6 assigns units and `per_firm_sales`, then rent collects at 6.5, care at 6.8, registered debt at 9.5, while `_batch_apply_household_updates` adds wages/transfers/taxes and debits goods at 10. The latter can subtract a full nonsubsidized goods cost after earlier payments; its capped-subsidy branch can scale household quantity after firm sales were recorded. Therefore a literal essential food → rent → care → debt → discretionary goods settlement order requires a **coordinated revision**: split essential from discretionary orders, bind each accepted allocation to actual available cash, and reconcile changed units, inventory, seller revenue, tax snapshot and subsidy before firm fiscal close. The essential-food candidate caps attempted units at `max(0, min_food_per_tick − usable opening food inventory)` and actual matched seller quantity/price; any unsupplied reservation is released before phase-6.5 rent. It is a priority to attempt an affordable purchase, never a food guarantee. A confirmed critical patient cost cannot outrank rent unless priced and authorized before 6.5 by an explicit timing revision. A voluntary precautionary cash buffer is the household withholding its own money under a chosen rule, **not** a provider claim, qualified slot or guaranteed authorization; defer it in the first pilot. Public coverage uses its separate envelope. If coordinated goods revision is too large, HH-P02 remains diagnostic-only and no role claims the written priority already settles cash correctly.

**Housing viability:** two further weekly cure opportunities following the first shortfall, including zero payment, can preserve shelter for a temporary earnings loss. The provider instead holds an unsecured receivable and may miss payroll or a phase-6.6b mortgage. Current rent first prevents a payment being consumed by old arrears while a fresh miss grows; a tenant with money can cure both, while a persistently cash-poor tenant exits with a larger write-off. The no-surviving-claim exit is viable as a bounded institution if tenant/provider claim mirrors, shelter and roster links close once and the write-off is reported as landlord loss, not cash or debt forgiveness revenue. It does weaken incentives to repay when exit is imminent; a surviving collectible claim would require a new named collector and priority, so should be a separate arm, not silently assumed. No relief without a separate prior-close cash envelope. One-tick warning is the low-state fallback, but answers tenancy timing rather than arrears accounting.

**Care viability:** retain one funding-denied episode rather than generate a fresh illness or charge an unrequested loan. At most one active episode ID per household and a saturating age counter bound state; retry at the next eligible request pass with no time-only or payer-based expiry. Close on treatment or household exit; defer clinical-recovery closure until its threshold is common across arms. This can prolong unmet need and lower provider receipts when the public cap empties. A requested episode may enter one provider queue before a slot opens; on a later funding denial remove that queue entry, retain the due episode outside the physical queue, and count funding denial separately from physical waiting. A patient-pay offer uses only cash and actually released deposits, plus one approved registered loan if that arm permits it; no-bank unregistered fallback cannot be called funded. Failure to assemble the full quote leaves no heal or receipt. Fallback: price-blind requests with explicit affordability/funding denials.

## Fable 5.1 disposition and remaining contract choices

**C01/C02, HH-P01/02.** I accept Fable's one-pass rent/registered-debt *planning view* but reject treating `cash + 0.9·deposit − dues` as a guarantee: bank reserves and earlier spending can deny withdrawal, the present phase-6.5 income/liquidity heuristic may still evict despite a phase-2 reserve, and a new lease signed at 6.5 has no known pre-plan rent. HOUSE-P02's pay-what-is-collectible rule would explicitly replace that heuristic in its own arm. Subtract obligations only once. Fable's “40 rent lowers budget exactly 40” applies only where the liquidity cap binds; if desired budget is already below it, planned spending may not move. At phase 10, derive the belief update from the *local ordinary* `wage_income` and `taxes_paid` that match the wage-tax snapshot, once after assessment. `last_wage_income` includes CEO salary (`backend/economy.py:1208`), while `last_other_income = -taxes_paid` (`:1210`); dividing those fields would dilute withholding and falsely turn tax into income. With no ordinary wage, retain the previous share or initialize from the public headline. Fable C02's hard `[0,0.6]` clip is too low: the top assessed rate can exceed 0.6 under the validated 0.50 headline and existing top scaler (about 0.615 with the default scaler). Bound the **belief** to a finite valid share such as `[0,1]` without altering progressive assessment or brackets. Prefer Fable's one-paycheck lag as the declared default and immediate announcement response as a sensitivity arm. The live batch planner is the economic reader; scalar parity remains desirable API hygiene.

**R2-HH-GOV-01 — response to R2-GOV-HH-01, C06.** Government's conditional Food-first candidate is viable for household comparisons *only* if the shared resolver actually settles a matched Food purchase and releases unsupplied cash before rent. A low-cash tenant can then eat but accrue rent arrears or miss a phase-9.5 installment; a cash-rich tenant with stock available can pay all claims and retain discretionary capacity. Scarce bank reserves or empty shelves block even the Food attempt. Cache cadence changes phase-2 desired budgets but cannot be allowed to reuse an old payment authorization after cash, dues or the actual withdrawal changes. This candidate is an institutional choice, not government approval or source behavior; planning-only C01 cannot enforce it. New care/rent/rebate envelopes must be nonoverlapping restrictions on prior-close free treasury cash. An earmark is not another asset or provider payable; paid baseline outlays are already reflected in closing cash, so subtracting them again from a free-cash stock would double count. Existing social/misc/bond routes and baseline transfers remain active or are explicitly changed in the comparison manifest. A phase-10 rebate in tick `t+1` authorized from close `t` cannot affect normal phase-2 purchases until **tick `t+2`**, and performance-cache refresh may delay the plan further. Pre-plan `t+1` payment is a separate, faster timing intervention. Neither is future phase-2 liquid income at `t`.
