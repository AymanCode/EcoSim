## Bank-owned mechanics and competing cash

At phase 5b, compute each household's combined known planned goods, weekly current rent, registered installments and at most one posted care quote against its **signed opening cash**, after any actually disbursed phase-2a consumption loan. Request only the positive shortfall, capped by 90% of the **opening** household deposit and by `BankAgent.withdraw`'s actual available reserves. Call that method once per household and reduce its deposit balance by exactly the returned amount; its aggregate deposit liability and bank reserves fall by the same amount. If cash starts negative, some release first fills that hole; a negative balance cannot buy Food. For this first slice, retain existing deterministic household iteration order rather than invent a batch liquidity auction. Early households can obtain more of scarce reserves than later ones; report requests, actual release and shortfalls by opening liquidity band. A later pro rata bank-wide withdrawal policy would be a different institution. Rent and care payment helpers must consume this cash without another `withdraw` call. Mortgage receipts at 6.6b and household repayments at 6.9 may refill reserves, but cannot reopen the 5b withdrawal pass. That is real temporal scarcity, not a deposit guarantee.

PS1's 6a matched essential Food, 6.5 current rent then arrears, 6.8 accepted/funded care, 6.9 registered household debt, and 6.95 residual goods is viable from the bank's perspective. At each step spend only current nonnegative cash and a specifically authorized subsidy/relief/medical-credit amount for its named recipient. Food and residual goods share one stock/price book; cheaper partial stock changes actual quantity and cost, not a promise to buy the original order. Each accepted goods or care purchase debits household/public/medical-loan cash once into Economy's tick-local clearing account and creates one named firm payable; phase 9 releases it to firm cash once. Failed or unaffordable attempts create no seller claim, stock decrement or tax base. Direct rent reaches landlord at 6.5 and is outside clearing. Households cannot spend phase-8.5 capital recycle, moved miscellaneous distribution, phase-10 wages/tax/transfers, or later dividends within this window. Goods/care expense at phase 10 is a receipt/ledger projection only, **never another debit**.

For each active registered **household** claim at 6.9, the bank builds one borrower index and derives its due by contract ID/version. Legacy v1 uses `min(payment_per_tick, remaining)` under its frozen full-term fee; v2 accrues that tick's contractual interest on outstanding principal **once**, then caps scheduled due at its remaining principal plus unpaid interest. A newly originated consumption or medical contract has `first_due_tick ≥ current_tick + 1`; no same-tick first installment. This does not retroactively change already-active v1 schedules. Provider-owned mortgages stay at 6.6b, and registered firm loans remain at 9.5 after firm sale cash is released. For a household with claims due `d_i`, residual collectible cash `C = max(0, cash_balance)`, and `D = Σd_i`, pay each `p_i = d_i × min(1, C/D)` when `D > 0`, with currency rounding remainder assigned in stable claim-ID order. This **pro rata scheduled-due** tie rule prevents an earlier bank or treasury contract from absorbing all cash by list order. A claim with `p_i < d_i` beyond declared currency tolerance records its own miss; partial receipt reduces due interest then principal and credits only its actual bank or treasury funder. A token payment does not erase a miss. No payment is taken for a zero-due, paid-off or not-yet-due claim. Unpaid interest stays a separate claim without interest-on-interest; write-off/discharge remains the explicitly versioned default institution, not a second cash transfer.

An accepted phase-6.8 medical loan is **purpose-bound** to the remaining price of that one qualified visit. Origination and full provider payable commit together; rejection or incomplete funding creates neither visit nor loan, while any earlier actual deposit withdrawal remains household cash. By contrast, an actually disbursed phase-2a **consumption** loan is unrestricted opening cash under current source. Treasury-funded medical credit is off in the first cash-closed pilot and, if separately enabled, debits its prior-funded emergency-credit envelope, never care coverage funds. Its repayment at 6.9 from the next tick onward goes to treasury; bank-owned loan interest funds bank deposit interest only when actually collected.

The bank loses some current-tick receipts because 6.9 collection precedes the delayed household cash from phase-8.5 recycling and phase 10. A cash-poor household may buy affordable Food and miss rent or loans; the miss is a lender claim/loss path, not a public bailout. With no Food stock, no payment/reservation is made and cash reaches rent/debt; with a failed deposit withdrawal, even Food may be denied. A cash-rich household can satisfy all dues if supply and quotes permit. Earlier 6.9 actual receipts can raise bank reserves before a Services upgrade loan at the combined market close. That loan may use **current** lendable reserves only after the ordinary reserve/buffer, purpose and prior-*settled*-revenue tests; escrowed current sales are not firm cash or underwriting history. Report changed approvals as PS1 timing effects. I choose actual collection over a 6.9 debt escrow released at 9.5: escrow would add a pending allocation and second claim clock while still barring 8.5/10 income from rescuing the installment.

## PS3 income-first addendum — supersedes PS1's late wage/benefit premise

**Bank verdict: accept PS3.** PS1's late ordinary wage/benefit premise is superseded. Only **actually paid** 5a net wages and baseline benefits enter signed cash before the one 5b withdrawal; unpaid wages and denied benefits never do. Withdrawal remains capped at 90% of opening deposit and actual bank reserves. The 6.9 collector sees cash left after Food, rent and care, without another withdrawal or catch-up. Later recycling, miscellaneous distribution and CEO pay remain unavailable.

**PAY3-BANK-FIRM-01 — answer to Firm's relayed PAY3-FIRM-BANK-01 (PS3/C03/C04).** The 5a cash cap is viable: after nonwage costs, positive firm cash plus loans disbursed before 5a pays current earned ordinary gross pro rata, then old named wage arrears pro rata, then a funded CEO hold. Clearing sale payables are unavailable. Full earned wages create expense once; unpaid amounts are paired firm payables/worker receivables. Old-claim payment retires liability without another expense. At exit, positive cash pays worker claims pro rata **before** unsecured bank, treasury and mortgage claims; unpaid worker balance closes as loss without a lender guarantee. Negative firm cash pays none.

**Credit-gate correction requested for PS3 §2/§4:** Carrying worker arrears cannot be hidden behind escrowed same-tick sales or a firm debt mirror. At each ordinary credit decision, use prior *settled* per-tick revenue, planned current payroll, known nonwage costs, **existing scheduled loan PMTs once**, and the recorded worker-payable balance. Treat the latter as a **senior liability stock** for leverage/runway and include the next declared arrears-cure amount in weekly payment capacity; do not subtract the entire stock again as a weekly expense. If no funded cure schedule is declared, deny new discretionary capital/entry credit while worker arrears are positive; a separately purpose-bound, reserve-gated working-capital loan can fund the current payroll/cure if prior settled capacity supports it. Loan proceeds must be actually disbursed before 5a, never advanced against phase-9 clearing receivables. Existing firm loans do **not** automatically default merely because wage arrears arise: their contracted due is tested against actual firm cash at 9.5, after phase-9 seller release. PS3 gives workers priority at each 5a payroll pass and at exit; it does not add a second wage sweep at phase 9 ahead of the 9.5 collector. That can leave old wages unpaid while a lender receives a phase-9.5 installment, an explicit cost to report. A stricter in-life worker-priority sweep would be a different timing contract and require corresponding withholding and worker receipt entries.

**PAY3-BANK-HH-01 — answer to Household's relayed PAY3-HH-BANK-01 (PS3/C01/C03).** Bank accepts post-5a **signed** cash, one 5b actual withdrawal, and residual 6.9 collection. Restricted wage withholding cannot fund a current public outlay; funded CEO cash is neither firm free cash nor household liquidity until phase 10. Prior-close benefit reserve B pays actual eligible households at 5a, pro rata if short; denied entitlement is no lender claim. Use the same cash-limited B and gap-fill-off rule in both timing arms, holding matched funded late-arm payments until 10. At 6.9 derive due once by claim ID/version: v1 frozen flat-fee `min(payment_per_tick, remaining)`; v2 accrues principal interest once, pays interest first and caps final payoff. New consumption/medical claims first fall due next tick. Allocate residual cash pro rata across bank/treasury dues with stable-ID rounding; each short-paid claim records a miss and credits its funder once. No wage promise, tax/CEO hold, second withdrawal or phase-10 replay can cure it.

These PAY3 replies are **addressed here for coordinator relay**; Firm and Household have not replied to this addendum. PS3 is a paper contract, with no implementation, simulation or benchmark claimed.

**PAY3-BANK-FINAL-01 — final §2 acknowledgment (Bank → coordinator/Firm).** Bank accepts the narrowed pilot: any recorded worker arrears blocks discretionary new capital/entry credit until cleared. A separately requested pre-5a working-capital loan may fund current payroll and a named cure only after actual reserve and borrower gates; count its installment and one-time cure once. Treat worker-payable stock as senior leverage/runway liability, never a full weekly expense. Existing firm debt may still collect at 9.5 while old wages await next 5a; arrears alone cause neither automatic default nor a new phase-9 worker sweep. This is a chosen tradeoff, not current source behavior.
