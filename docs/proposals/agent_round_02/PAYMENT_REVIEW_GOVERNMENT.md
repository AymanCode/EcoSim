## Verdict: PS2 needs PS3's paid income before bills

I accept one **5b withdrawal → 6a matched essential Food → 6.5 current weekly rent then old arrears → 6.8 accepted care → 6.9 actual registered household debt → 6.95 residual goods** window, followed by combined sale/tax close at 7/9 and receipt-only household processing at 10. The source currently debits firm wages at phase 5 but credits household wages at 10 (`backend/agents.py:5265–5282`; `backend/economy.py:1187–1206`); goods allocation precedes the phase-10 household debit and can disagree with later affordability (`backend/economy.py:3836–3980,1230–1285`). PS2 repairs the sale/debit mismatch, but the late wage credit still makes workers pay bills before they receive pay for the work just performed. I recommend **PS3 income-first** as a bounded amendment, with actual funding rather than a forecast or an overdraft.

After phase-4 roster synchronization and phase-5 production, Economy settles ordinary payroll at **5a**, before the single 5b deposit withdrawal; the current phase-5 aggregate wage cash debit is removed. For firm `f`, compute gross wage due from its actual worker roster. Firm cash plus already disbursed working capital, after nonwage production costs, funds `paid_f = min(gross_due_f, max(0, firm_cash))`. Pay workers proportionally to gross due, with stable worker-ID rounding; debit the firm `paid_f` once. Unpaid due is a named worker wage-arrears claim, not household cash or tax revenue, payable later or at worker-priority exit. Firm profit may accrue the **full earned** wage only because the unpaid portion is a liability; do not deduct it again on later payment. This exposes firm distress and promises no full wage.

At 5a run the existing progressive wage-bracket calculation on **gross wages actually paid**, including zeros for unpaid employed workers, rather than nominal `household.wage` (`backend/economy.py:4344–4357`; `backend/agents.py:6895–6920`). For each worker, firm gross debit = worker net cash credit + actual tax withheld. Put withheld cash in a restricted wage-tax holding balance: treasury money increases once, but its free cash does not rise until phase 11 releases the restriction. Phase 7 reuses this settled wage-tax record while calculating firm taxes; phase 10 records wage/tax receipts and beliefs without a second household credit/debit, and phase 11 recognizes withholding as revenue **without adding cash again**. `last_wage_income` and tax beliefs use paid gross/tax; a promised but unpaid wage is never a tax payment or 5b liquidity.

**Baseline unemployment benefits also settle at 5a, but this is a changed institution.** At the *prior* fiscal close, after existing paid outlays and nonoverlapping carried payables/restrictions, let `F = max(0, signed treasury cash − those restrictions − reserved payables)` and `B = min(benefit_level × unemployed_count_at_that_close, F)`. Internally restrict that real cash; do not create another asset. After next tick's labor match, identify actually unemployed households and pay the lesser of the announced baseline entitlement and B divided pro rata across eligible IDs, with stable rounding. Debit B and credit households once; phase 10/11 only report it. If more people are unemployed than anticipated, each receives less, and the unpaid balance is a denied benefit, **not** a treasury debt or landlord claim. If fewer qualify, unused B remains restricted until the next fiscal close, then releases once. Existing baseline transfers can currently make treasury cash negative (`backend/agents.py:6802–6875,7010–7044`); PS3's cash-limited baseline is an explicit pilot change, not a claim that current benefits are funded. Apply that same cash limit and keep gap-filling transfers off in **both** income-timing comparison arms; otherwise a difference cannot be attributed to timing. Re-enabling gap-fill needs a separately prior-funded cap. Phase-8.5 recycling, moved miscellaneous distribution, dividends and healthcare bonuses cannot reopen the window.

The source pays CEO salary separately in phase 10 and omits it from the household wage-tax snapshot (`backend/economy.py:1173–1206,4344–4357`). Keep it **late management income** in PS3. At 5a, after ordinary workers, hold at most the existing 3×median-worker amount from positive remaining firm cash; phase 10 releases that funded hold to the CEO once, with no second firm debit. Deduct only the funded amount in the firm tax snapshot. It is not 5b liquidity; taxing CEO pay as ordinary wages or creating unpaid CEO claims would be separate changes.

## Public cash and sale settlement

Care, rent and generic-sector subsidies must draw from **different named restrictions** on the same treasury cash. After B is funded, optional care-only or rent-only comparison arm uses `A = min(0.10 × prior-tick actual collected tax, max(0, F − B))`; 10% is a bounded, uncalibrated scenario default. The selected destination gets 100% of A; an explicitly enabled mixed arm fixes 50% care/50% rent. Reserve arm leaves A free. Optional rent relief and treasury emergency credit are **off by default**; emergency credit, if ever compared, needs its own prior-funded share and registered treasury claim. Previously restricted care/rent cash cannot pay B, and phase-5a withholding or this tick's later taxes cannot fund any 6a–6.95 program. These priorities and shares are candidate institutions, not user-approved fiscal preferences.

Care-only eligibility is one active due episode per household in a qualified provider's queue order. At 5b the **covered/no-self-pay arm quotes zero patient care charge** for withdrawal; a public-only bill must not ration bank reserves. Only the separate patient-pay arm quotes at most one newly requested **or already queued** due visit, without reserving a slot. At 6.8 fix the full posted quote after a qualified slot; covered care reserves its whole price from care cash and, on completion, moves it once into Economy clearing for the phase-9 provider payable. Failed delivery releases to **care** only. Empty funding removes the offered patient from the physical queue but retains the due episode outside it; no automatic debt or self-pay switch. Patient-pay uses remaining cash after Food/rent and a fully approved purpose-bound bank loan if needed. Set both healthcare-targeted sector subsidy and configured visit-share to zero for the **same visit** in both clean arms. One delivered visit has one payer path, not unlimited free service.

If another generic sector subsidy remains active, at window opening reserve `G = min(0.05 × trailing GDP, 0.02 × positive unencumbered treasury cash, that unencumbered cash)` once, using the existing cap coefficients (`backend/fiscal_guards.py:91–97`). Its source function currently reads raw cash at tick reset, so PS2 must instead exclude B/A, any older earmark, wage-tax hold and payables; otherwise the same money can be promised twice. For locked price `p>0`, planned/available quantity bound `Q`, household spendable cash `H=max(0,cash)`, subsidy share `s∈[0,1]` and remaining G, accept the **greatest** `q∈[0,Q]` satisfying `p q − min(s p q,G) ≤ H`. Commit household share and actual public share to clearing, plus stock/throughput and provider payable, once. When G empties, shrink the *sale itself* to the affordable quantity; never allocate the original seller sale and shrink only the household's phase-10 quantity (`backend/economy.py:1100–1120,1230–1300`). Care visits remain indivisible and require full funding. Unused G/B/A restrictions release only at the next fiscal close, with **no extra cash credit**.

Rent relief, if explicitly enabled, requires an active tenancy frozen at intervention with pre-policy `max(0,cash)+0.9×deposit` below one weekly contract rent and a verified 6.5 current shortfall. In stable tenant-ID order, pay landlord directly against remaining current due, then named old arrears, capped at **one weekly rent total per tenant** and the remaining rent envelope. Cancel the matching tenant claim once before 6.6b mortgage service. This can reduce notices and support lender payment, but gives no mortgage guarantee and can crowd out covered care or other treasury uses. Food-first can lower tenant rent cash, increasing relief requests and lender misses even when calories improve. The direct landlord receipt is already firm cash; add `paid_rent_for_tax` once to the phase-7 housing profit snapshot and the missing property fields (FP04), while phase 9 must not credit the firm that rent again. The current `_build_firm_tax_snapshots` sees generic sales only (`backend/economy.py:4360–4409`); without this correction, a claim that PS2 relief raises profit/property tax receipts would be unsupported.

The shared two-pass goods/care clearing balance equals the sum of fully funded firm payables before phase 9 and zero after its one release. Phase-7 tax and phase-9 profit use the **combined committed** lines, frozen transaction prices and direct-rent tax record. Household phase 10 consumes receipts only: no second goods/care debit, inventory addition or late subsidy shrink. Earlier net pay and B can increase Food, rent, care and 6.9 debt service, but can also exhaust firm payroll cash or prior treasury reserve. Report these opposing outcomes, bank withdrawal denials, signed cash gaps and both execution modes; do not infer welfare or a calibrated multiplier from nominal sales.
