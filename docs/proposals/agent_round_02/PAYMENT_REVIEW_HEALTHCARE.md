A queued/requested patient *can* pay from actually credited net income after Food and rent. Government reserves baseline transfer cash at prior close: `B = min(benefit_level × then-unemployed count, positive free close)`, before care `A` below; at 5a it pays actual eligible unemployed pro rata when short. Unused B stays restricted until next fiscal close. Care/rent restrictions cannot fund it; withheld wage tax stays restricted until 11. Phase 10/11 reads records without repeat cash movements. Late capital recycle, moved miscellaneous distribution, deposit interest, bonuses and dividends cannot rescue a 6.8 denial.

At 5b, request **one** actual bank withdrawal per household using PS2's signed-cash shortfall, the 90% opening-deposit ceiling and `BankAgent.withdraw`'s actual release. In the patient-pay arm include the posted quote for at most one active **newly requested or already queued** due visit; excluding an old queued visit denies long-wait patients normal deposit access. In the covered arm, care adds **zero** to the patient withdrawal request because this pilot never converts cap exhaustion into self-pay. The quote is a cash-access estimate, not escrow. Known zero qualified capacity adds no phantom visit quote; if a patient-pay slot later fails, unused withdrawn cash stays with the household. No housing or care helper may withdraw again this window, even after 6.6b mortgage receipts refill reserves. Current `_ensure_cash_for_payment` and `_process_healthcare_services` both allow later withdrawals; PS2 replaces those branches. Negative signed cash persists; only positive cash remaining after Food and rent/arrears is patient spendable at 6.8.

Use the existing provider assignment and one physical queue entry per due episode. In this pilot, count only employed doctors/residents' `medical_visit_capacity` with fractional carryover; unqualified or missing roster IDs supply zero, and a provider with no qualified slot leaves the patient in the *physical* queue without quote, charge, receipt or healing. Keep the current sick-doctor priority and deterministic rotation common across payment arms; broader triage/training is outside PS2. A funding-denied patient leaves the physical queue but retains the same due episode ID outside it for next-tick retry, avoiding a false capacity wait or second illness draw. Denial does not consume a qualified completion slot: scan the next already queued patient within this tick's integer throughput. Funding-wait IDs must not inflate later queue-based surge pricing or arrivals as fresh illness.

For the **covered care-only arm**, at prior close restrict `A = min(0.10 × actual collected tax, max(0, free closing treasury cash after B and other carried claims))` model currency; compare with leaving A in reserve.
