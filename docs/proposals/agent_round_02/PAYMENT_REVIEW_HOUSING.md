## Verdict and exact housing settlement

I **conditionally accept PS2's exact funded order**: Food at 6a, current weekly rent then old arrears at 6.5, qualified/fully funded care at 6.8, actual registered household debt collection at 6.9, and residual goods at 6.95. A failed Food match spends nothing and leaves cash available for rent. The shared goods book must commit household/public cash, quantity, seller payable and inventory/throughput together; both passes and care close before phase-7 tax snapshots. Generic seller cash stays in the clearing account until phase 9, while rent is a direct landlord credit at 6.5. Phase 10 reads purchase receipts without a second debit. This selects a concrete payment institution, including its adverse distribution; it is more than a phase-2 planning reserve.

**PS3 is compatible when income is actually funded before 5b.** After phase-5 production, pair each funded ordinary gross wage with one firm debit, a worker net-cash credit and an actual treasury withholding credit at the same boundary; cap or deny a wage that the firm cannot fund under the chosen payroll rule. Government's concrete prior-close baseline-benefit reserve is `B = min(benefit_level × then-unemployed count, positive free cash after existing payables/restrictions)`; pay only actual after-labor eligible recipients at 5a, pro rata if their count rises, and debit that reserve once. Optional rent relief draws only from free cash **remaining after B**. Gap-filling transfers are off and CEO pay stays at 10 in this cash-limited first pilot. At 5b the once-only requested shortfall covers planned gross goods, current rent, old arrears, registered dues, at most one requested posted care quote and the bounded applicant quote below, minus resulting **signed** household cash, capped by 90% of opening deposits and actual bank release. No phase-10/11 repeat wage, tax or baseline-transfer cash posting is allowed. This gives a paid worker or benefit recipient more cash for Food/rent than PS2's late-income draft, but a cash-strapped employer or treasury may pay less than the old implicit schedule. A new tenant is admitted only after its **first full weekly rent** is funded after Food; an incumbent can pay partially. A future expected wage or authorized but unpaid transfer cannot sign or cure a tenancy. Current income may reduce rent arrears and relief draw, but does not guarantee them; late misc distribution, capital recycle, CEO pay, bonus and dividends do not reopen this window.

**Unhoused applicant's one quote.** The current 5b rent field is zero for an unhoused household, so omitting housing from its withdrawal request would unnecessarily exclude a deposit-rich, cash-poor applicant. Build one 5b provider pass over *currently vacant* Housing units and take the cheapest **prior settled posted weekly ask** as a nonbinding applicant quote; add it to each currently unhoused household's combined 5b request. This reuses the housing-provider view, with no per-applicant firm scan and no unit reservation. At 6.5, match against the actual available unit/ask and require its full first rent from residual cash; the applicant may fail if the quoted vacancy disappeared, its price changed, Food consumed cash, or bank reserves denied the withdrawal. If no unit was vacant at 5b but an eviction opens one later, no quote is added and that tick's applicant can sign only from its remaining cash. Unspent withdrawn money remains the household's cash, while early speculative draws can ration later depositors; report both effects. No second withdrawal is allowed.

At 6.5, the housing resolver reads the existing active tenancy's stored `monthly_rent` as **weekly contract due**; this focused review does not choose a new ask writer or cap. Let `C = max(0, signed cash left after 6a)`, `D = current_due`, `A = old_arrears`. Debit household/credit landlord `min(C,D)` for current rent, then `min(max(0,C-D),A)` for old arrears; keep a paired tenant liability/provider receivable for the unpaid remainder. A negative stored cash balance stays negative and produces zero tenant payment. The single actual 5b bank withdrawal already changed cash/deposit liability; rent, arrears and care must **not** call `_ensure_cash_for_payment` or another bank withdrawal. Purpose-bound medical credit and restricted public care money cannot pay rent. A Food subsidy, if used, affects only the accepted Food line and cannot be counted again as household cash.

First end-of-6.5 unpaid arrears serves notice; **two further weekly 6.5 opportunities** follow. Full cure by tenant cash plus any actual relief cancels notice. Otherwise terminate immediately after the second subsequent settlement, release the unit for other applicants that tick, exclude that departing household from immediate rematch, clear both roster links and write off the paired unpaid rent claim without creating cash. Provider exit clears its tenants/receivables by the same paired rule. Zero payment follows the same clock. Shelter remains met while the tenancy is valid during notice. This fixed two-opportunity rule is a chosen first-slice assumption; a provider-runway override is a separate policy. Existing clearing instead evicts on a wage-plus-liquidity heuristic before payment and can withdraw again; both paths must be replaced for PS2, including its eviction-gap metric reset. Provider payroll is paid before rent and may already be short under PS3; rent/relief cash can support later mortgage service at 6.6b, but tenant arrears and a provider mortgage miss are **different claims** with separate counters and funders.

**Cash-only relief, default off.** In a rent-only arm, restrict at the prior fiscal close `A_public = min(0.10 × actual collected tax receipts, max(0, free closing cash after B and existing outlays/restrictions/payables))`; 10% is an uncalibrated scenario setting. Eligibility is an active tenancy at intervention with frozen pre-policy `max(0,cash) + 0.9 × deposit` below one weekly contract rent, plus a verified current shortfall at 6.5. That frozen liquidity number classifies eligibility; it is **not** spendable cash. Process eligible tenants by stable household ID. After their own current-rent/old-arrears payment, pay a direct treasury→landlord grant of at most **one weekly contract rent per tenant per tick**, limited further by the remaining restricted envelope, against remaining current shortfall first and named old arrears second. Debit restricted cash, credit landlord, reduce the paired tenant claim once. A partial grant leaves notice/arrears active; no mortgage guarantee or new-entrant subsidy is implied. Unused restricted cash returns to free treasury only at the next fiscal close, never same-tick care/projects. Reserve comparator keeps that amount free; a mixed arm needs fixed distinct shares, not arrival-order raiding. The larger two-rent cap is a labeled sensitivity, not this first rule.

## Source changes, adverse cases and exchange

Source presently allocates generic seller revenue in `_clear_goods_market` before phase-10 household spending; `_batch_apply_household_updates` can later scale a subsidized quantity. PS2 must replace those paths with its one funded clearing book, and phase-9 `apply_sales_and_profit` must bypass a second stock subtraction. Housing rent remains outside generic `per_firm_sales`. Government accepts a once-only `paid_rent_for_tax` receipt in the phase-7 housing profit snapshot and the missing housing fields for property tax (FP04); today `_build_firm_tax_snapshots` omits both, so present tax does **not** already reflect direct rent/relief cash. Phase 9 must not credit that receipt to firm cash again. With cash 65, funded Food 20 and weekly rent 50, rent payment is 45 and paired current shortfall 5; care/debt/goods see no household cash unless a separate named payer funds them. With no Food stock, that 20 is not spent and rent can receive 50. Earlier real wages/transfers can change those numbers; partial bank release still caps them. More Food-first payment can preserve consumption while raising landlord arrears, provider mortgage misses, public relief demand and possible later housing loss. PS3 early funded income can reverse some of that, at the cost of exposing firm/treasury inability to pay. Report both directions by pre-policy tenant and provider cash bands.

This is a design acceptance, not an implementation claim.
