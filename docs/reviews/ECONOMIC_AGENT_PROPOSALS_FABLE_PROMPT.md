## Purpose and restrictions

EcoSim should help compare policy changes through realistic, affordable abstractions: progressive taxes, patient-paid versus publicly paid healthcare, price/inflation mechanisms and eventually explicitly specified economic institutions. Realism means preserving the relevant decision, information, resource constraint, counterparty, timing and feedback. The model cannot calculate all of real life; more detail is useful only if it changes the intended comparison interpretably.

- Review only. Do not write/edit files, code, tests, scripts, configuration, dependencies, shared rules or the original proposals. Do not run the simulator, benchmarks, tests, installs, shell commands or code execution. Do not launch agents, services or live LLM economic actors. Do not commit, publish, deploy, send messages or alter credentials/access.
- Read only the supplied repository snapshot and relevant public primary economic sources through the provided read/web tools. Do not seek secrets, personal files, `.env`, OAuth material, unrelated projects or account information. Source access here is sufficient; it does not require GitHub permissions or making the repository public.
- Preserve each base economic actor's role. The goal is better decisions and interactions, not wholesale replacement or an ideological success bonus. Keep progressive taxation; replacing it with a flat tax or redesigning brackets is not a main task.
- Individuality must be consequential: stable, bounded traits and identities plus changing cash, debt, jobs, ownership, health, skills and experience should reach actual decision readers. Similar choices under a binding constraint are allowed. Avoid cosmetic random traits, privileged IDs, forced uniqueness and a separate model call per household or firm.
- Preserve practical speed. Favor shared behavior code, bounded state, existing arrays/indexes and small choice sets. Identify population-by-firm or borrower-by-loan scans, unbounded histories and cumulative costs. The shared 5% median/p95 target is a project engineering threshold, not a user-supplied number, a measured pass or a per-proposal allowance. Normal and performance-mode cadences differ economically.
- Current defects and weak abstractions are challengeable. Distinguish binding rules from descriptions of current behavior and known limitations. A requested shared-rule revision is not already approved, but it is not automatically an invalid idea either.
- Keep source findings, economic hypotheses, chosen simplifications and executed evidence distinct. No new experiments have been performed in this proposal round. Do not claim calibration, runtime validation, empirical policy-effect sizes or preferred GDP/ideology outcomes.

## Questions to answer

1. **Would this improve the intended policy comparison?** Identify the missing mechanism each proposal repairs and the simplest sufficient abstraction. Challenge invented realism, large expansions unrelated to the question, and unsupported parameter choices. Is the rule neutral about desired aggregate results?
2. **Does each factual premise match the live source?** Trace called paths, decision inputs and side effects. Check scalar, batch and cached execution, factory initialization, actual trait readers, policy-schema/runtime exposure and observation timing. Separate planned offers, allocations and settled payments. A named method or config field is not proof it is active.
3. **Does policy transmission reach the rest of the economy coherently?** Trace payer/recipient, first behavioral response, affected workers/customers/owners/lenders/providers, delays and subsequent tax/price/health feedback. Include an adverse effect and a case where the chain stops. Avoid assuming benefits reach everyone or that an aggregate average describes all households/firms.
4. **Does the accounting close?** Distinguish stocks and flows; principal and interest; cash, deposits, income forecasts, encumbrances and creditor claims; due versus paid; bank versus treasury funders; opening endowments versus funded entrants. Check partial payments, refunds, arrears, loan mirrors, default/write-off and exit. One obligation gets one settlement owner; no unfunded receipt or double benefit.
5. **Are scarcity and information respected?** No future wages/tax receipts/loan approvals spent early; no private/future state in choices without a stated institution. Public payment does not create doctors, goods or housing. Queues, denial, funding exhaustion and physical capacity require different observations and failure rules.
6. **Can the six proposals coexist?** Look for duplicate reactions to taxes/benefits/rates, conflicting price/claim writers, multiple uses of the same bank/treasury cash, incompatible funding priorities, inconsistent loan schedules and new obligations ignored at exit. Identify minimal shared contracts and sequencing before parallel implementation.
7. **Is the evidence appropriate?** Use primary research/institutional sources for technical/economic claims if checking the web. Distinguish observational/theoretical evidence from causal evidence; no universal coefficients from one setting. A primary source motivates a mechanism, not all consequences of a proposed regime. Identify material unverifiable claims and citation limits; do not call an access-blocked page a broken reference if an original paper is otherwise available.
8. **Would a comparison identify what it claims?** Check financing/revenue-use closure, common initial ownership and RNG state, matched exogenous inputs, endogenous divergence, pre-policy subgroup definitions, normal/performance mode, capabilities, price versus quantity/welfare measures and observation phase. No benchmark, empirical validity or full checkpoint branching is established by a manifest alone.
9. **What is genuinely concrete versus still a choice?** Separate source defects/accounting corrections from behavioral hypotheses and institutions such as lease terms, triage, fiscal funding, bank money creation and labor responses. Identify what can be specified next without redesigning the economy and what should be deferred.
10. **Turn vagueness into a proposed workable mechanism.** For each proposal marked revise, needs evidence/modeling choice or defer, do more than say it needs specification. Recommend a minimal rule or clearly bounded first slice, with state/units, decision information, trigger, phase, payer/recipient, resource gate, exhaustion/default/exit behavior, interacting actors and a discriminating later check. Give a simpler fallback and an assumption requiring empirical sensitivity or user selection. You may group a shared contract once and link all affected IDs. A material unresolved institution should have a recommended candidate and a credible alternative with tradeoffs; do not silently present your preference as user approval. If a full extension is too large, specify the useful reduced version and exactly which questions it cannot answer.
11. **Prepare actual actor negotiation.** For every consequential cross-role contract, name the originating role, roles whose budgets/choices/claims change, a concrete question each counterpart must answer, likely benefits/objections and a candidate compromise. Examples: a housing grace period changes household cash, landlord rent receipts, bank mortgage risk and treasury relief priorities; public care changes patient charges, provider capacity, bank medical credit and government funding. Ask for viability under cash-poor/capacity-bound as well as favorable cases. Preserve dissent and rejected alternatives; inter-agent agreement is not source proof. The coordinator handles communication outside this restricted CLI session, so do not spawn your own agents.
