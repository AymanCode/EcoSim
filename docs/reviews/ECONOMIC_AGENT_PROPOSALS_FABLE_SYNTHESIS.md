### C01 — Household obligations view and payment priority
*IDs:* HH-P02, BANK-P02, CARE-P01, HOUSE-P02. *Roles:* Household, Bank, Housing, Care, Government, Firm.

**Agreed constraints.** The view is a desired-demand estimate, never escrow or a bank promise (Bank `R2-BANK-HH-01/03`, Household §"Fable disposition"). Registered dues are subtracted once; the bank supplies a per-borrower index built once per tick; provider-owned mortgages are excluded (Bank §BANK-P02). The Food → current rent/arrears → accepted patient-paid care → registered debt → discretionary order is a **candidate institution** viable only with atomic goods settlement, because phase 6 records seller sales (`backend/economy.py:1872`) and phase 10 debits without a cash check (`:1275`). Until then HH-P02 is diagnostic (Household `R2-HH-BANK-02`, Housing `R2-HOUSING-HH-02`, Government `R2-GOV-HH-01`, Bank `R2-BANK-HH-03`). Care objects to any care claim over already allocated goods (`R2-CARE-HH-02`). Care requires the view **before** request enqueueing (Care §C01/C02).

**Rule.** Build once per tick at entry, immediately before `_enqueue_healthcare_requests` (`backend/economy.py:1529`). Per household, ephemeral arrays in model currency per tick: `due_rent = monthly_rent` (weekly, despite the name); `due_installments` = sum of `payment_per_tick` over `bank.active_loans` with `borrower_type == "household"`, indexed once (O(L), the pattern at `:6693–6698`); `accessible_deposits = 0.9 × max(0, bank_deposit)` (estimate matching `:851` and `:3759`); `planning_liquidity = max(0, cash + accessible_deposits − due_rent − due_installments)`. Readers: the phase-2 batch planner uses it in place of `accessible_liquidity` (`:852`) as the drawdown base; CARE-P01 reads it at the request decision. Writers of cash are unchanged. Education spending at phase 2 (`:1693`) happens after the build and makes the view slightly optimistic; disclosed, not corrected.

**Denial/exit.** Obligations above liquidity give a zero plan; existing eviction and miss paths remain visible. No top-up.

**Recommended assumption.** Ship the planning-only slice. The strong form (atomic reservation at phase 6 with essential-Food priority) is a shared market revision under M01 that changes `_clear_goods_market`, `settle_capped_subsidized_goods_purchase` and firm tax snapshots; it should be a separately specified arm. **Alternative:** implement the strong form first; tradeoff is one large resolver change against measured food deprivation in cash-poor tenants.

**Later check.** Two households identical except rent 40 differ by at most 40 in planned budget, and only where the liquidity cap binds (Household's correction to my first report). **Cost:** O(H + L) per tick; no persistent state.
