# Coordinator audit of Fable's W06–W18 designs

**2026-09-21. Verdict: useful design material; accept selected directions, revise several claimed-ready specifications.** The [original Fable proposal](ECONOMIC_DESIGN_FABLE_PROPOSALS.md) is preserved verbatim. Its [prompt](ECONOMIC_DESIGN_FABLE_PROMPT.md) and [run manifest](ECONOMIC_DESIGN_FABLE_RUN.json) identify the model and frozen baseline. This audit evaluates it against the implemented [W01–W05 package](../ECONOMIC_CONCRETE_PACKAGE.md), authoritative source, and primary references. None of W06–W18 has been implemented by this audit.

Fable made the choices much more explicit: funding sources, ownership, metrics, tax expectations, fiscal responses, experiment timing, small institutional comparisons, and dependencies. Its recommendations to preserve progressive brackets, separate ownership dimensions, retain scarce healthcare capacity, avoid an immediate input-output-sector rewrite, and label assumptions are sound. Its readiness labels are too optimistic in several places.

## 1. Corrections required before implementation

| ID | Proposal / severity | Problem and evidence | Required correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | W06/W12 signed cash and overdraft creation — blocking | W06 defines M using **signed** treasury cash. Paying 10 to a household while treasury cash moves from 0 to −10 leaves that M unchanged. W12 then logs the 10 overdraft increase as a cash-creation line, making the audit residual −10 despite exactly matched flows. This follows directly from the proposed equations. | Maintain separate concepts: a signed settlement checksum, gross spendable balances, and debt/claims. Choose an explicit monetary-authority boundary and reconcile its asset/liability when calling something creation. `max(0, −treasury_cash)` is a useful analytical financing-gap stock; it is not a substitute for a creditor's recorded claim. Do not add its change to the signed-cash checksum. [S1] |
| D02 | W06 exit losses and owner funding — model choices still open | Assigning an exiting firm's negative cash to an unrelated bank's reserves closes an arithmetic sum but invents a liability for the bank. Unequal equity contributions followed by equal dividends also transfers value among owners; assigning free shares to loan-funded entrants is another distributive choice. `_ensure_cash_for_payment` can return false after a partial withdrawal if bank liquidity is insufficient. | Identify any overdraft creditor at origination, or use a separately declared legacy-resolution account. Make contributions and rights explicit (equal commitments or weighted shares). Funding must preflight **actual available cash/reserves**, then settle atomically or roll back every partial withdrawal. Do not debit a household below its available funds after ignoring the helper's result. Future exit settlement must use W01's funder-aware helper, not assume every bank-serviced loan belongs to the bank. |
| D03 | W08 multiplicative tax expectations — blocking | A zero initial expectation remains zero when multiplied by a new/old nominal-rate ratio. The zero-rate fallback assigns everyone the headline rate, ignoring bracket scalers. Two setter calls before planning overwrite the proposed rescale: 0.15→0.20→0.40 leaves a factor of 2, although the total change is 8/3. Bracket membership can also change. Source: `GovernmentAgent.plan_taxes`, `set_lever`; both consumption planners. | First specify a shared, side-effect-free expected-tax calculation using the existing progressive rule and a declared information set. For announcement mode, calculate the new schedule once per planning boundary; do not multiply stale effective rates. For learning-only mode, define startup, unemployment/reemployment and zero-income behavior. Separate the information assumption from settlement. Keep scalar/batch inputs equal for this new signal, while documenting their existing saving discrepancy. No bracket redesign is required. |
| D04 | W07 rolling income and flow classification — blocking for income poverty | An exact trailing four-tick mean cannot be maintained with one float per household: the value leaving the window must be known. The proposed income sum also counts W06 liquidation payments classified as `dividends`, while omitting deposit interest currently mixed into the `bank` ledger. Calling all of it disposable income overstates its completeness. | Use a four-slot ring buffer plus a running sum, or explicitly choose an EMA and rename it. Define startup coverage. Separate capital distributions, loan principal, deposit principal and interest from recurring income before making an income-poverty measure. Additive liquid-resource stocks are a smaller ready slice; income metrics need this definition work. |
| D05 | W07 price/output extraction — blocking for the full metric package | `per_firm_sales.revenue` can include a government subsidy; it is not necessarily household-paid spending. Food units, visits, and occupied rental units require separate matching denominators. No reference price exists for a category with no reference-window transactions. Free-at-use care can have a zero consumer price, which is valid for a consumer-cost measure but unsuitable as a universal production or investment deflator. | Specify settlement-derived producer receipts, household payments and physical quantities separately. Freeze common positive reference prices/weights before treatment; define zero sales, zero user prices and absent categories. Keep consumer-cost indices, producer prices and constant-price output distinct. Consumer CPI and an investment deflator answer different questions. [S2] |
| D06 | W10 replay, RNG, and fingerprint claims — revise | N5 incorrectly says there are no NumPy RNG calls: `_get_purchase_tie_break_noise` uses an explicitly seeded `np.random.default_rng` and caches arrays. `to_dict()` surfaces omit internal state, so equality of those dictionaries alone does not prove full state equivalence. A cached mutable per-tick RNG can reintroduce order dependence. | Prefer two isolated deterministic replays for the first diagnostic, with independent configuration contexts. Inventory all state, configuration, cached arrays and RNG surfaces; fingerprint a declared set and state its limits. Test stable entity/purpose keys directly, not whether a finite set of first draws happen to differ. Include runtimes in evidence. Different firms may have different outcomes after treatment without breaking paired exogenous disturbances. The existing `policy_forecasting/determinism.py` can be reused for output checks, but is not a complete state fingerprint. [S3] |
| D07 | W11 delayed public capital — blocking | The proposed `deque(maxlen=D)` followed by append then popleft can silently discard the oldest cohort on append and shorten the queue. Its cost index is the W07 consumer basket, and its payment recipient does not necessarily provide real inputs. A delay and a multiplier bound alone do not establish resource feasibility. | Define spending at t, delivery at a specific later phase/tick, and production availability explicitly. Use one due-date slot per cohort or pop before append with proven timing. State the investment-price assumption and capacity/input constraint. Keeping a reduced-form stock is reasonable, but label its supply abstraction and sensitivity; it is not yet an L02-complete investment market. |
| D08 | W12 rebate integration — incomplete | The proposed rebate credits cash and the ledger, but does not explicitly debit the treasury or specify its inclusion in `tick_spending` / `_update_budget_pressure`. `last_rebate_income` has no specified consumer in the current planner. A negative B* and negative treasury cash can still produce a positive rebate under the formula. | Specify an atomic treasury debit/household credit, fiscal-flow classification and timing. Choose whether borrowing may finance a rebate when cash is below zero. Name how households learn this income and whether it affects their next plan; writing a new field does not create that behavior. Distinguish the rebate instrument's rules from a complete fiscal closure. |
| D09 | W13 comparison changes two institutions — revise | Only the public-payer arm receives administered prices. The result then reflects both financing/access and provider-pricing rules. Indexing administered healthcare prices to a basket that contains healthcare can be circular unless lag/sequence is fixed. | Use common provider pricing in the first payer-only test, run pricing as a separate factorial dimension, or explicitly label the comparison as a package. Choose a lagged/excluding-healthcare index if indexation is needed. Preserve queue/capacity/training mechanics; paying per completed treatment with no patient charge is a useful first design. Verify both config subsidy and sector-lever settings in the patient-pay arm. [S4] |
| D10 | W17 empirical screen has wrong sign — correction | Fable lists **positive unemployment–vacancy co-movement** as a stylized fact. The conventional Beveridge relationship is inverse; shifts can occur, so neither sign should be hard-coded as a universal requirement. | Describe the relationship, measurement and horizon correctly, and treat it as a diagnostic rather than an ideological or mechanical success test. [S5] |
| D11 | W06 diagnostic significance gate — revise | Making a known missing counterparty a required correction only when a pilot confidence interval excludes zero can excuse invalid accounting because the run is noisy or short. A tolerance proportional to the whole money stock can also hide systematic smaller errors. | Separate exact accounting acceptance from materiality prioritization. Use scale-aware numerical tolerances for individual transactions plus aggregate residual checks. Use magnitude/sensitivity analysis to prioritize explicit abstractions, never to excuse an unexplained internal transfer. |

The tool trace contains one failed Grep (unsupported regex lookbehind). I independently inspected the RNG sites; this is one reason not to accept N5 as an exhaustive search result.

An additional wording correction: the W09 inventory calls `_adjust_government_policy` a policy chooser. Its actual callee, `GovernmentAgent.adjust_policies`, currently performs transfer-budget sizing only. Use the executed code rather than that older orchestration comment. A source-grep test would not establish that toggles preserve economic capabilities; use focused behavior checks.

## 4. First tax comparison: retain the design, repair the prerequisites

Keep 15% versus 40% as **headline rates within the existing progressive schedule**, with the same bracket scalers and other rules. Record actual effective rates; this is not a flat-tax experiment. A common pre-period, declared intervention boundary, two fiscal closures and paired seed results are appropriate. Fable's 62-tick intervention, 104-tick horizon and seed-count bounds are assumptions, not calibrated recommendations or proof of adequacy.

Before drawing policy conclusions, complete D01–D08 as applicable and define the outcomes and fiscal assumptions. The existing W03 outputs already expose the start-of-run policy timing, source hashes, configuration, failures and metric limitations, but do **not** yet implement intervention branching, fiscal closures, basket indices or net-wage planning. W01/W02 remove the verified repayment errors; they do not reconcile all other money flows.

For numerical precision, specify absolute tolerances for outcomes near zero as well as relative tolerances. Choose primary outcomes and the stopping rule before inspecting favorable signs. Report uncertainty, transition paths and assumption dependence. Longer histories should be chosen using a common pre-treatment diagnostic, not selected separately after seeing a preferred arm's post-treatment result. No composite winner or ideological performance bonus is warranted.

Reproducible equation/input checks: [verification script](verify_design_counterexamples.py) and [results](evidence/ECONOMIC_DESIGN_COUNTEREXAMPLES.json). They demonstrate D01, D03, D04, D07 and the N2 input difference; they do not estimate aggregate policy effects.

## 5. Primary-source checks

The following support principles or specific corrections, not any proposed numerical coefficient. Original proposal references remain available in Fable's catalog; unverified items retain its access limitations.

- **S1:** [Bank of England, Money creation in the modern economy](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-creation-in-the-modern-economy). Supports distinguishing money, bank deposits and matched balance-sheet claims. EcoSim's prefunded lender is a simplified institution, not this article's complete banking system.
- **S2:** [BLS CPI questions and answers](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/questions-and-answers.htm) and [BEA real GDP FAQ](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/79), also verified in the initial audit. Support separating consumer price measurement from real output; neither validates a heterogeneous posted-price mean as CPI. [OECD poverty definition](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/poverty-rate.html) supports the half-median relative-income convention. Household-size and equivalisation differences must be disclosed.
- **S3:** [Python 3.13 random documentation](https://docs.python.org/3.13/library/random.html). Confirms that version-2 string seeds use all bits and independent generator instances have separate state. It does not promise that this application's different purposes are independent or that all higher-level algorithms are unchanged across Python versions. Source-hash and runtime evidence therefore matter.
- **S4:** [WHO health financing](https://www.who.int/health-topics/health-financing) distinguishes raising revenue, pooling and purchasing. [RAND's Health Insurance Experiment summary](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_briefs/RB9174.html) supports the relevance of cost-sharing to use, including both more-effective and less-effective care. It does not supply a universal coefficient for EcoSim or establish the outcome of its free-care scenario.
- **S5:** [BLS Beveridge curve](https://www.bls.gov/charts/job-openings-and-labor-turnover/job-openings-unemployment-beveridge-curve.htm) and [BLS JOLTS chart notes](https://www.bls.gov/web/jolts/jlt_labstatgraphs.pdf). Correct the sign of the conventional unemployment/vacancy relationship; shifts and unusual episodes mean this is a contextual validation diagnostic.

**Audit limits:** source and equation review plus focused correction probes; no W06–W18 implementation, causal policy-effect estimate, full RNG-independence proof, external calibration or comprehensive citation replication. The original report's claim that a feature is cheap remains an estimate until the integrated feature is benchmarked.
