## Findings

**PSF01 (blocking). Two wage records still disagree after the frozen book.** Source fact: each tick the phase-4 sync rebuilds `firm.actual_wages` from `household.wage` whenever that wage is positive, at `economy.py:3695–3702`, and continuing workers carry `household.wage` into their outcome at `economy.py:3033–3037`. Firm-side wage changes are written only to `firm.actual_wages`: the fifty-tick raise at `economy.py:3614–3622`, the phase-9 cut in `adjust_wages_to_revenue_ratio`, and the healthcare minimum reset at `agents.py:5475–5479`. All three revert at the next sync. Proposed rule: PS3 §2 freezes `actual_wages` at phase 5 and forbids reconstructing pay from `household.wage`, but never writes anything back. Result: the payroll book can pay a gross that the household record never shows, while labor planning, consumption budgets at `economy.py:795` and the consumption-loan leverage test at `agents.py:1528` keep reading the stale field. Correction: add one sentence to §2, "after freezing the book at 5a, set `household.wage` equal to that worker's frozen gross due, and keep paid and unpaid amounts only in the claim record." The alternative, making `actual_wages` the single source and reversing the sync direction, is larger and touches labor matching.

**PSF02 (blocking). Owners can be paid before unpaid workers.** Source fact: `distribute_profits` at `agents.py:5710–5745` pays dividends whenever `net_profit` is positive and cash exceeds six weeks of costs, and `distribute_healthcare_worker_bonus` pays from positive cash at `agents.py:5779`. Neither reads any worker claim. Under PS3, `last_tick_total_costs` holds full accrued wages while the cash outflow was smaller, so a firm that short-paid at 5a looks cash-rich at 16. Proposed rule: §2 calls worker payables a senior liability and orders workers before owners at exit, and it names the 9.5 loan leakage as an explicit tradeoff, but says nothing about phase 16. Correction: in the PS3 arm, distributable cash in both routines becomes `max(0, cash − safety_buffer − outstanding worker payables)`. Add that sentence to §2 and to the Firm and Household acceptance rows; no role has read it yet.

**PSF03 (blocking). The fiscal close has no phase number.** Proposed rule: B, A, G release, "then-unemployed count" and "actual tax receipts" all hang on a "prior fiscal close" that §1 and §5 never locate in `Economy.step`. Source fact: exits set `employer_id` to `None` at `economy.py:4534`, after phase 11, and entry recomputes the unemployed count at `economy.py:1547` before labor matching, so the count differs by phase. Correction: declare the close as the point immediately after `_update_budget_pressure` at `economy.py:2060`. Count B eligibles as households with `employer_id is None` at that point. Define the A base as that tick's released withholding plus phase-9 profit, price-ceiling and property debits, and state whether the phase-11.6 investment tax at `economy.py:2045` is included. Tick zero appropriates at the first `step` entry before `_apply_post_warmup_stimulus`. Choosing the end of phase 16 instead is acceptable, but one choice must be written.

**PSF04 (required choice). Employed, unpaid and ineligible.** Source fact: exits fire on cash below the threshold or twelve ticks of cash at or below zero, measured after revenue in `apply_sales_and_profit` at `agents.py:5351`. PS3 removes the payroll overdraft, so a firm with a trickle of sales resets its streak every phase 9 and never exits while paying partial wages. Its workers stay employed, receive no wage, and are not "actually unemployed", so B excludes them. §2 flags duration as a limitation but does not state the benefit interaction. Correction: choose one and write it. Either add an exit trigger, "worker arrears at or above twelve weeks of current gross due", or state that unpaid employed workers remain benefit-ineligible and report their count and duration each tick.

**PSF05 (correction). Exit recovery is a new mechanism, and recoveries will be near zero.** Source fact: `_handle_firm_exits` lays off workers, removes the firm with its remaining cash, and writes off bank loans at `economy.py:4529–4571`. Nothing is distributed to anyone. Both triggers imply nonpositive cash, so §2's worker-first waterfall will rarely pay. Correction: state that recoveries are computed before `write_off_loan`, that the household claim survives the `household.wage = 0` write at `economy.py:4535`, and that expected recovery is approximately zero under current triggers.

**PSF06 (correction). Order inside the adapted phase 9.** `apply_sales_and_profit` sets `zero_cash_streak`, debits taxes and calls `adjust_wages_to_revenue_ratio` in one body. PS3 says Economy credits clearing cash "once" but not whether before or after that call. Write: credit clearing cash, then tax debit, then streak, then metrics. The wage cut inside that call is covered by PSF01's write-back.

**PSF07 (correction). Two treasury debit sites are missing from §5's guard list.** Source fact: new-firm seed cash at `economy.py:4743` and treasury-funded firm loans through `_issue_firm_loan`, reachable from the phase-1 working-capital bridge at `economy.py:2484`, debit raw cash. §5 names subsidies, public works, stimulus, emergency credit and investments but not these. Add both. Note the bridge is issued at phase 1, so it does qualify as "disbursed before 5a", but its treasury share must consume free cash after B and A.

**PSF08 (limit). Services posted-price write.** `_clear_goods_market` raises `firm.price` to the effective floor during clearing at `economy.py:3812`. §3 freezes effective prices but does not say whether that write is kept. State: keep it once at book build.

**PSF09 (limit). Rollback presumes a capability that does not exist.** §5 relies on "a saved complete pre-arm state". Source has only `copy.deepcopy` for the LLM snapshot at `server.py:1446`, and `HouseholdAgent.to_dict` at `agents.py:1240–1304` omits tenancy and consumption-loan fields. Restate rollback as re-initialization from seed and config under K07's RNG discipline.

**PSF10 (limit). Phase 10 still debits firms.** Firm government-loan servicing at `economy.py:1160–1170` is neither replaced nor named in §1's phase-10 row. It stays a firm cash debit after 9.5 and before exits and dividends; name it.
