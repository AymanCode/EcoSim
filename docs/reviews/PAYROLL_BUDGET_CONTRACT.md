## Problem and evidence

Current payroll settlement follows production and caps wages at employer cash. Unpaid amounts become explicit firm-worker claims; current wages are paid before older claims. Household planning forecasts full contract take-home pay and learns taxation, not wage-payment reliability. A controlled actual-method example expects 100, receives 80, records 20 owed and still expects 100 next time. Checkout remains cash constrained, so this is an information/timing gap rather than authorization to overspend.

One exact 520-week replay shows the aggregate claim stock mixes old baseline Food, Healthcare and private firm balances. It does not isolate which employers caused the increase versus another research model. Evidence is external under `payroll-affordability-followup/`. The new rule prevents future private unfunded work; it neither deletes old claims nor purports to solve public-provider finance.

These are owner-selected employment/payment institutions and accounting constraints, not calibrated behavioral coefficients. Working-capital finance allows receipts and payroll to occur at different times; it cannot guarantee repayment or manufacture lender cash. The current bank's underwriting rules remain the lending hypothesis. No new waiting period, rate or risk threshold is calibrated by this change.

## Selected private employment boundary

Apply only to ordinary private Food and Services, excluding both baseline flags/registrations and medical-only workers. Baseline providers and specialist Housing/Healthcare labor are preserved. On the funded payment paths, check final gross payroll after matching, incumbent wage-floor enforcement, roster repair and scheduled raises, before ordinary work history, skill updates, wage freeze and production.

If employer cash covers payroll, introduce no financing or layoff. Otherwise request one bank-funded working-capital loan using the existing 52-tick term, working-capital spread and per-firm maximum, bounded by the actual cash gap and existing leverage capacity. Respect `working_capital_enabled`, current bank reserves, credit score, cooldown and prior-settled repayment-income/combined-installment underwriting. No unemployment/growth-worker-gap condition is imposed on this emergency-payroll purpose; it is distinct from the existing expansion bridge. No Treasury fallback, repeated search, loan forgiveness or weakening of the bank's debt-service share. Registered origination debits bank cash and updates its claim/dues indexes and firm mirrors; the caller credits the approved principal to firm cash once. A smaller approved request may support fewer contracts; denial makes no cash/debt change.

If funding is insufficient, cancel newly matched hires first (reverse matching order), then use the existing stable incumbent roster/layoff order until the remaining complete contracts fit cash. This is a bounded deterministic affordability rule, not a new worker-value optimizer. Contracts are not prorated or cut retroactively. Zero workers is allowed when no complete contract can be funded. Previously earned claims remain due under their existing repayment/exit ownership.

Update both employment mirrors, actual wage maps/caches, resolved hire/layoff outcomes, final actual-hire/unfilled diagnostics and labor events. Cancelled hires must not appear as successful hires or accepted-offer diagnostics. Dismissed incumbents retain a financial-layoff event. Preserve original plans separately from final outcomes. Public Services installation must require a retained funded additional hire; a cancelled hire creates no project payment or slot. Private production is bounded by the retained workforce's existing capacity formula.

Financial Services cuts reduce its retained staffing target and reset recovery evidence without destroying installed space. At zero workers, retain permission for one affordable restart rather than requiring sales evidence that zero workers cannot produce. Further growth retains the existing three strong-sales observations. Financial inability to fund work is distinct from the existing five-observation weak-demand layoff patience rule. No baseline staffing, clinic wage/bonus or public support changes.

## Labor application and state ownership

Split household labor application into contract assignment and its existing work-history/expectation body; keep the existing public method as a compatibility wrapper. Stage only households resolved to private Food/Services, synchronize rosters, apply existing scheduled raises in its current firm/roster/RNG order, then enforce funding. Finalize each deferred household's history once from its final employed/unemployed state. Other households retain their existing history application, including medical repair ordering. Apply the shared skill update after final employment. The narrowly intentional private expectation change is that it sees the raised pre-work contract rather than a pre-raise wage. Raise cadence, amounts and eligibility are unchanged; wage-affordability reform remains parked.

`PaymentBook` remains the settlement owner. A bounded tick-local set marks firms that passed this pre-work check; settlement rejects a later current-pay shortfall for those firms rather than silently creating a new claim. Direct settlement helpers still handle old states, tests and baseline-provider shortfalls. Current implementation has no cash production input charge between the gate and payroll; future changes that spend that cash must protect the same funding boundary.

Per-firm decision diagnostics record current payroll required, opening available cash, bank credit requested/funded, cancelled hires, financial layoffs and retained workers. Values are current-tick model currency/counts, overwritten each tick; no new household debt state or history. No household-by-firm scan or employee optimization search.

## Receipt-based shopping boundary

Keep labor planning before matching. Move the standard flow's single shopping decision to after `PaymentBook.settle_income`, tax observation and funded benefit settlement, before deposit quotes/withdrawals and the first goods pass. Reuse the same prepared market snapshot, price/quality utility, awareness pools, order allocation, subsidies and purchase ordering. Normal mode decides every tick; performance mode keeps its existing complete-plan five-tick refresh/reuse cadence, with actual receipt inputs on refresh and actual-cash constraints at every purchase. No new re-shopping or extra awareness refresh. The later-pay comparison keeps its earlier planning point and cannot use held wages/benefits.

For a settled budget, read ordinary net wages and benefits from this tick's payment book, including genuinely funded older wage receipts. CEO money remains held and cannot enter shopping. Current cash already includes those received amounts: do not add them again to the liquidity ceiling. To derive the separate savings/other-opening-liquidity drawdown, subtract this tick's credited wages and benefits from cash before applying the existing accessible-deposit and obligation rules. Preserve signed cash, once-indexed rent/debt, healthcare reserve, MPC traits and separate dividend treatment. Forecast helpers remain available for later-pay and direct callers; an explicit settled-input call requires completed income and benefit settlement. Planning moves no money.

Consumption credit request/approval still occurs before the eventual shopping decision; approved cash is available, while loan principal is not wage income. Existing payment/claim collectors, tax settlement and recipient ownership remain unchanged.
