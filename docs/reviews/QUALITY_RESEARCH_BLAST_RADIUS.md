# Research change boundaries

QUALITY-RESEARCH-01 changes private Food/Services starting products and research. The unchanged `instant` default is a compatibility control. This page distinguishes executed checks from remaining experiment gates.

| Boundary | Selected change and retained owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| State and timing | One optional typed project; full paid installments advance it at phase 11.6. Pauses retain progress; quality arrives at completion. | 95 focused project/factory/cash cases and 24 real-tick integration cases. |
| Starting product | Basic baseline quality; keyed initial and private-peer entry draws. Founder skill affects the initial draw only. | Non-quality factory fields, owners and global RNG compared across seeds; four later-founder cases. |
| Workers and obligations | Earned costs, funded old wages, CEO pay, taxes and actual collected debt reduce discretionary earnings. Existing collectors retain payment ownership. | Both named sequences, final real bank and Treasury payments, positive profit with old payroll/CEO payments, unpaid claims and zero sales. |
| Research recipients | Actual debit drives investment tax and the existing miscellaneous service/household route. Failed research buys an attempt. | Per-step cash, tax, pool and clearing reconciliation; next-tick beneficiary effects. |
| Customers | Quality flows through the unchanged technology multiplier and seller utility. No extra search, demand knowledge or purchase cadence. | Existing snapshot/household tests plus the matched economy panel. The panel records raw and effective quality separately. |
| Firm survival and entry | Exit discards a project without refunds. New entity identities distinguish reused numeric IDs. | Entry-focused tests and external identity-epoch lifecycle observation; panel records spending abandoned at exit. |
| Specialized sectors | Housing/Healthcare keep their existing paths. No parked Housing capital change is included. | Exclusion cases, unchanged default-path tests and source diff against main. |
| Runtime and reporting | O(firms + registered loans) additional work, bounded project state and at most two rolling gain records. No household-by-firm scan. | Algorithm review, full-suite gate and separate repeated normal-mode timing. Instrumented macro timings are exploratory. |

Research protects its cash boundary, not cash after later dividends. The six-week debt reserve is a capped schedule estimate and does not forecast unaccrued interest. These limits remain visible in the review and results.

The economic comparison uses 1,000 households, three matched seeds, both named payment sequences and 520 weekly ticks per arm. Its four arms are original baseline, scale-only, project-outcome and project-cost. Scale-only changes starting quality and removes instant Food/Services research together; it does not isolate starting quality alone. Report late weeks 421–520 alongside endpoints, real delivery/access/jobs and wellbeing alongside nominal GDP, and each seed rather than only averages.

The throughput decomposition keeps four explicit checkpoints: first version the shared rules and interface; then parallelize disjoint design, cash-boundary review and external harness preparation; serialize all shared implementation writes; finally separate frozen-source economic runs from repeated timing. One lifecycle and one settlement adapter serve both alternatives. Independent review does not become a second writer.
