# Food and Services quality and research

Ticket QUALITY-RESEARCH-01, rules v1.18, source main `8a8d38005d914681ad95bc82d6b3523793be03be`. Owner approved the behavior and local implementation/comparison on 2026-10-27. All numeric settings below are initial simulator experiments. They are not empirical calibration.

## Definition of done and scope

Deliver two executable project-research alternatives, a scale-only control and the original baseline. Verify the common invariants through real `Economy.step` transitions, compare matched economic outcomes through at least 500 ticks and report adverse effects. No household utility, awareness, re-shopping, wage, capacity, baseline retirement, Housing or Healthcare change. The earlier `codex/firm-investment-returns` branch remains parked.

The initial `firms.quality_research_model` default is `instant` to preserve baseline scenarios while comparing candidates. Values are `instant`, `scale_only`, `project_outcome` and `project_cost`. The default is a disclosed compatibility choice during this experiment. A report must not describe an opt-in candidate as the default production model. Invalid models are rejected by config validation. No UI or new policy lever is added.

## Bounded data shape and writer map

A new `backend/firm_research.py` owns the project lifecycle and entry-quality distributions. One optional typed `ResearchState` on `FirmAgent` contains an optional `ResearchProject`, project sequence, a 13-observation cash-generation EMA, last processed tick, completion/failure counters, last spend/gain and at most two `(tick, gain)` records. A project stores start tick/quality, required and funded work ticks, fixed installment and spent amount, with active or paused status. An absent project means idle. No unbounded history or household search is added.

`FirmAgent.advance_research(*, tick, operating_cash_generation, operating_costs=None, available_cash=None, blocked=False) -> float` is the single firm-facing writer. It commits a returned funded installment to firm cash and accumulated investment, and a completed gain to quality. It returns actual spending for existing recipient settlement. Duplicate calls in one tick spend nothing and advance nothing. Defaults for optional arguments use the firm's own current costs/cash. The method is for the selected project models; the existing instant method remains the baseline and the unchanged Housing path.

Factory creation and spontaneous entry call the shared new quality sampler only for Food and Services under non-instant models. Existing non-quality RNG draws remain consumed in their original order; separate purpose/entity-keyed local RNGs determine new quality and project outcomes. Initial quality is adjusted after factory ownership assignment, so founder skill can influence the draw without changing ownership or unrelated traits. Research never reads founder skill. Do not reseed global RNGs.

Food/Services baselines have quality 0.1 under the new models and skip both instant and project research. First private firms use a 99% centrally concentrated beta draw in [0.11, 1.11] and a 1% beta upper tail in (1.11, 4.99). Founder skill gently changes the central distribution, never its support. Later entrants sample around the active private category median, exclude baseline/PublicWorks peers and stay above baseline. The same bounded founder influence shifts their central beta draw, not the industry's frontier or ongoing research effectiveness. Their upside shrinks as median quality rises and cannot exceed the best existing private peer. With no peers they use the first-generation draw. This last ceiling is a documented diffusion simplification, not a claim about real-world entry innovation.

## Funding and timing

Keep phase 11.6 as the only research spending/advance phase. Call project research for eligible firms even at zero sales so the project can pause and its earnings estimate can decay. Housing keeps its existing revenue-gated instant path. The new Food/Services models never use the old payment sequence's research-overdraft convention.

At phase 9.5, when a project model is active, capture eligible firm cash before registered and direct debt collection. After household settlement capture actual debt debit and post-debt cash in a tick-local dictionary. Named sequences have no other firm cash movement in that block. The old payment sequence can claw back seller subsidies there; subtract the recorded sales-revenue reduction from the cash reduction so clawback is not mistaken for debt. Do not modify debt collectors or reconstruct final payments from schedules after payoff.

For named sequences, conservative retail cash generation is settled goods receipts minus `max(last_tick_total_costs, funded_wages + nonwage_cash_cost)`, funded CEO pay, actual profit and price-ceiling tax, and observed debt debit. This deducts full earned wages and also cash paid on older wage claims. Current public installation receipts and previously paid assessed receipts are not new retail earnings. Gate wage arrears and use the pre-installation post-debt cash as an additional available-cash ceiling. For the old payment sequence use the now-settled current sales/profit record, known CEO payment and observed debt, explicitly documenting its overdraft convention outside this project path. No opening cash or new loan principal is earnings.

The EMA uses alpha 2/14, starts at zero and updates once per tick, including losses. Allowance is the lesser of `(0.15 + 0.25 * bounded_risk_tolerance) * max(0, EMA)` and cash above a six-week operating-cost-plus-future-dues reserve. Current actual cash and the recorded post-debt cash both cap discretionary funding. Include known CEO operating pay in costs. Bank infrastructure dues are already in the bank mirror; never add them again. Bound future schedules by outstanding balances for the reserve; observed debt, rather than that reserve estimate, enters earnings.

Installments are fixed at project start. A tick advances only when its full installment is affordable and the firm has no blocking distress or wage arrears. Otherwise the project pauses with its progress and spending unchanged. Completion returns to idle. Exit drops the project without refund. Quality changes only at completion and does not decay under these sector project models.

## Two implementations

Variant A, `project_outcome`, costs 1,200 over 40 funded ticks. Failure probability is 15%. On success, gain is `0.35 * (1-q/10)^1.5 * min(3, exp(N(0, 0.5)))`, using quality at the project start. Gains shrink across the scale. The 52-tick cap also applies.

Variant B, `project_cost`, costs `1200 * exp(0.25*q)` and takes `round(36 + 2*q)` funded ticks. At completion 10% fail, 85% gain `0.25*(1-q/10)` and 5% gain 2.5 times that amount. Quality therefore raises both effort and duration, and lowers gain. These are two economic alternatives over one lifecycle, not separate settlement implementations.

At completion remove gain records at or before `tick - 52`, limit total remaining plus new gain to 1.0, and bound resulting quality to [0,10]. Minimum project duration is 36 funded ticks and one project exists at a time, so at most two completion records are live. Outcome randomness is keyed by seed, stable firm identity and project sequence, not by the completion tick or execution order. Pausing does not reroll an outcome.

## Counterparties, compatibility and evidence

Actual spending debits the firm once, increments lifetime R&D spending once, credits existing investment tax once and sends the after-tax remainder through `_collect_misc_revenue`. The miscellaneous tax and later household beneficiaries remain current abstractions. A failed project buys an uncertain research attempt, not guaranteed quality. Less baseline or private spending can lower those household and Treasury receipts, while conserving firm cash and possibly increasing dividends. Better quality can redirect existing customer demand and future jobs; no aggregate benefit is guaranteed.

Preserve the existing float quality field, database schemas, payment ownership and phase order. Add serializable bounded research state and aggregate experiment counters without changing UI behavior. Normal/performance modes retain their existing cadence. No live LLM calls are added.

Prove completed gain timing, pause/resume, idempotence, no free progress, cash reserve and annual gain cap through focused tests. Prove named income timing and real debt payoff, subsidy clawback and wage-arrears handling through integrated tests. Verify non-quality factory traits/owners/RNG and Housing/Healthcare paths are preserved. Confirm household market snapshots receive the new raw quality times the unchanged government technology multiplier, while actual purchases remain funded. A source writeup alone is not a blast-radius proof.

The experiment starts with baseline versus scale-only, then both project variants, using common source/config/seed and preserved non-quality initial profiles. Scale-only is a frozen-quality reference: it changes the initial scale and removes instant Food/Services research together. Its contrast with the original baseline does not identify a pure initial-quality effect. The two project variants share the same new initial scale; their contrasts with scale-only include research benefits, costs and recipient transfers. A versus B compares whole research alternatives, not the isolated effect of one coefficient. Record intended quality differences rather than demanding identical overall initial states. Report income_first first and income_late second, raw/effective quality distributions, research funding/completion/failure/pause, entry/exit, business sales/profits/cash/debt, funded wages/jobs, units/access, household cash/health/happiness, GDP and accounting residuals. Use multiple seeds and a late window; separate finite-run evidence from calibration. A >=500-tick quality-only probe cannot substitute for economy evidence.

Use repeated representative 1k/10k normal-mode timing and bounded state estimates for computation evidence. Report uncertainty against the existing cumulative 5% budget. Preserve source, config, RNG, artifacts and rejected attempts. Research work is O(firms + registered loans) at worst, with no new household-by-firm scan. Evidence results and independent review are separate from this contract. Approved OpenWiki refresh is attempted only if available without Actions; otherwise mark it due.

## Economic basis and assumptions

[OECD Frascati Manual, chapter 2](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2015/10/frascati-manual-2015_g1g57dcb/9789264239012-en.pdf) supports uncertainty and systematic research effort. [Brown and Petersen, Cash holdings and R&D smoothing](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0929119910000040) supports financing/liquidity links, not these budget coefficients or a complete-stop rule. [Andrews, Criscuolo and Gal, frontier firms and technology diffusion](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/frontier-firms-technology-diffusion-and-public-policy_5jrql2q2jj7b-en.html) motivates uneven diffusion, not a literal private-sector median rule. [Bloom, Jones, Van Reenen and Webb](https://www.nber.org/papers/w23782) examine declining research productivity across several settings, not this simulator's salon/food quality scale, cost curve or success rate. The quality scale, yearly gain ceiling, entry distributions and absence of automatic decay are owner-selected abstractions. No real-world growth forecast follows from these experiments.
