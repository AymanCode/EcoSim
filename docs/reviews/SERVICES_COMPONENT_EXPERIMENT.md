# Services component experiment

Owner direction (2026-10-26): isolate the cause of the SERVICES-01 decline and keep the investigation bounded. This is a diagnostic experiment, with no additional production rule or parameter change.

Question: does the short-horizon decline come principally from stable pricing (P), the staffed/sustained-demand expansion gate (E), retained staffing and three-week recovery (H), or their interaction?

The screen uses all eight on/off combinations of these three existing mechanisms, three matched seeds (42, 43, 44), 1,000 households, 80 weekly ticks, 10 warmup ticks, normal mode and `income_first`. This is 24 runs and three independent seeded trajectories per combination. It targets the population where the previous package comparison showed the largest decline. `income_late` and 10,000-household generalization are outside this first screen; any confirmation will be limited to the identified mechanism. No patience, utilization, tax or customer parameter sweep is included.

External, frozen diagnostic copies select the original or implemented method bodies. P includes the desired-markup capture and consistent measured-markup denominator, with the original stabilization institution. E includes both bank gates, observation timing and evidence resets. H includes retained staffing, three qualifying observations, matcher enforcement and installation's staffing lifecycle. A shared slot-installation helper composes the two independent lifecycles: staffing reset belongs to H; space-shortage reset belongs to E. Inactive new data fields are not economic interventions. The all-off and all-on copies must reproduce the original main and complete candidate endpoints from the preceding panel before component findings are accepted.

Observers read completed state and existing plans without rerunning decisions, consuming randomness, changing customer knowledge or reducing agent cadence. Record initial profile and random-state fingerprints, source hashes, plans versus staffing, installed slots, production, completed sales, prices, wages, cash, debt/arrears, household employment/receipts/wellbeing, clearing cash and injection-adjusted money-stock residuals. Preserve per-firm identities and weekly timing. Observer neutrality is checked against the preceding uninstrumented endpoint cases for main and the complete candidate.

The early screen isolated pricing as the likely driver in seeds 42 and 43. One paired seed-42 replay (original versus pricing-only, two additional runs) observes the existing loan functions' return branches to distinguish a financing gate from lower customer purchases. It introduces no new treatment or economic setting and must reproduce both endpoint state and final random state from the screen. It is diagnostic instrumentation, not performance evidence.

Report paired endpoint and late-window changes across the three seeds, main effects and interactions. Repeats are not independent economic trajectories. A lower nominal GDP alone is insufficient: inspect service quantities, customer access, jobs, pay, household wellbeing and firm finance. An adverse aggregate does not establish a software defect or justify altering a selected rule. Use the trace to select one next design question; do not expand into a general business redesign.
