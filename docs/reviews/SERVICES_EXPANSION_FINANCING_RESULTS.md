## Behavior

Both existing Services expansion routes on `income_first` and `income_late` can use firm cash plus a loan, or either alone. Existing project cost/slot rules, staffed/sustained-demand eligibility and subtype restrictions remain. Contribution is cash above an existing personality/runway reserve, including estimated expanded payroll and balance-capped bank/Treasury dues. A partial-loan quote conservatively reserves the full-project installment; see the contract for the exact formula. Firms already below that reserve contribute zero and retain the old bank-only underwriting possibility.

Required borrowing still passes existing credit/funding/35% repayment checks. Denial spends no contribution and delivers no slots. On approval, bank reserves fall only by borrowed principal; firm cash falls only by its contribution; the full cost follows the existing route's recipients exactly once. Long-term spending still uses the implicit capital-goods/equal-household route, and infrastructure spending still uses the miscellaneous pool/tax route. No new builder, supplier or installation delay is claimed. Installed slots permit later recruitment, not instant employees. Project delivery uses PR66's shared target/evidence reset.

Household behavior, wages, R&D, startup terms and lender standards are outside this financing edit. Old-sequence financing remains bank-only. Fully cash-funded named projects can proceed without a bank; the source clock now invokes infrastructure funding even in that case.
