B. Sign off on the direction

What you'd be approving, in five lines.

  • Newcomer first. Friendly names, a sentence and a picture for every number. The analyst grid behind a link.
  • Two identical towns as the unit of everything. One to four of them, live, 1,000 households each by default, up to 10,000 shared.
  • The Run screen and Set up as mocked up. Businesses and households are first-class.
  • AI mayor mode judges the model: what it saw, asked, was allowed, said, and what happened.
  • Backend widening first, then screens, then AI, then saving.
