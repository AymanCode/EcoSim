    How to read this for first-timers

    • A tick is one week. Both towns run the same week at the same time, from the same starting point, so any difference you see is caused by the policy.
    • Minimum wage is the lowest weekly pay a firm may offer. Raising it puts more money in workers' pockets and more cost on firms. Which effect wins is what the towns are testing.
    • Every house is 20 households. Green ones have someone working, amber ones are looking for work, red ones have lost their home. Click a house to meet a family.
    • Every building on Main street is a firm. Taller means more staff. A flag means it's struggling to pay wages or rent, a cross means it closed.
