Town A is run by an AI mayor, Llama 3.3 70B on Groq. Town B is the same town with its policy frozen. Three years in, the AI has made five decisions. Fewer people are out of work in Town A, but the town hall owes half as much again.

The AI mayor's decisions

Every 26 weeks the AI gets a report on the town and may change up to two things. It never sees individual people or firms, only town-wide numbers, a couple of weeks old and slightly rounded. Code checks every request: it caps big moves and rejects anything outside the rules.

    How to read this for first-timers

    • The two towns are identical until the AI's first decision in week 15. Every difference after that is something the AI caused.
    • The AI is an advisor with a rulebook, not a dictator. It proposes, code checks. Capped means it asked for more than the rules allow. Rejected means the request broke a rule.
    • The evidence check compares what the AI said about the town with what was actually true, so you can tell good reasoning from lucky guesses.
    • Save this run and try another model on the same town to compare mayors.
