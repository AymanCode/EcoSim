Notes for review (not part of the app)

  • new numbers come from metrics, not curated: happiness, top10Share, bottom50Share, gdp, govRevenue, govTransfers. Showing them needs a backend change.
  • Happiness and the two shares are counted every 5 weeks and repeated in between, like the gap between rich and poor and the savings ladder. gdp and govRevenue are fresh every week; govTransfers is fresh too, and only repeats while it is 0 during setting up.
  • The count of unpaid loans (bankDefaultsTotal) is empty in both recordings, so that tile adds up the money written off each week instead.
  • The dashed "Example" row in Rules in force is made up to show the pattern; nobody changes a rule in the demo. The People out of work tile is shown in its hover state.
  • Data: the recorded demo (seed 1337, 500 households, 104 weeks), extracted by extract_numbers_data.py. Town B raises the minimum wage; nothing else differs.
